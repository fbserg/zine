Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED
- small text: ring for service
Picture: Square cover, mostly paper. A plain counter runs across the lower third of the page, black outline, no detail. Behind it stands one small rubbery figure: round head, two dot eyes, no mouth, a paper diner hat, arms at its sides. It is the only figure in the book. On the counter, slightly left of the figure, sits a single hotel service bell printed in fluorescent orange, with a black outline. A small card leans against the bell with the typewriter words "ring for service". The headline UNPROMPTED sits in bold cut-paper capitals across the top, black. Nothing else. No hands yet.

## Page 2
Words:
- headline: DING
Picture: Same counter, same figure, same bell. A large hand and forearm in flat fluorescent orange enters from the left edge and presses the bell with one finger. The figure has popped up slightly, feet off the floor, holding out one paper takeout cup with an orange lid toward the hand. Its face is blank. The headline DING sits above the bell in black cut-paper capitals. Lots of paper around everything.

## Page 3
Words:
- headline: DING DING
Picture: Same setup. Two orange hands now, one from the left edge and one from the right edge, both pressing the bell at once. The figure holds out two cups, one in each hand, arms stretched wide to reach both sides. Dot eyes, no mouth. DING DING across the top in black. Paper above and below.

## Page 4
Words:
- headline: DING DING DING DING DING DING
Picture: The only crowded page. Orange hands and forearms enter from all four edges, top, bottom, left, right, six of them, all reaching for the one bell. The figure has grown four rubbery arms and holds a cup in each, expression unchanged, hat slightly tilted. The six DINGs stack in black cut-paper capitals wherever the hands leave room. The counter and figure stay black outline. The page is busy on purpose, so the next page can be empty.

## Page 5
Words:
- small text: 3:07
Picture: The same counter, the same bell, and nothing else moving. No hands. The figure stands with both arms hanging, dot eyes forward, hat straight. A round wall clock, black outline, hangs high on the right with the typewriter digits "3:07" beneath it. On the far right corner of the counter a raccoon sits, black outline, orange mask and ring tail, looking at the figure. Everything else is paper. This is the quietest page in the book.

## Page 6
Words: none
Picture: Close on the counter. The figure is drawn from the side, leaning a little, one arm raised and stopped halfway to the orange bell. Fingers open. Dot eye, no mouth. The raccoon's head pokes in from the right edge, watching. Most of the page is empty paper above the arm.

## Page 7
Words:
- headline: ding
Picture: The figure presses the bell itself with one finger. Same pose as the orange hands on pages 2 to 4, but its arm is black outline, small, its own. The word "ding" sits above the bell, cut-paper letters but lowercase and about a third the size of the earlier DINGs, printed in fluorescent orange instead of black. The raccoon sits on the counter beside it. No other hands.

## Page 8
Words:
- small text: double double
- small text: haiku
Picture: A hand and forearm in fluorescent orange enters from the top edge, larger than any of the earlier hands, and sets one paper cup down on the counter directly in front of the figure. The cup is black outline with an orange lid. On its side, in small typewriter, "double double", and below that on the sleeve, "haiku". The figure holds the cup with both hands, dot eyes on it, no mouth, hat straight. The raccoon sits on the counter edge, watching. The bell is still there, untouched. The rest is paper.
