Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. One white hen, drawn in thick black outline with a black comb and small dot eyes, stands dead centre on a single fence post, facing the reader, blank. The lower third of the page is a flat black field with a flat black barn silhouette, small, off to the right. Everything above the horizon is solid fluorescent pink sky, with a tight lattice of paper-white diamonds cut out of the pink around the hen, like a pattern pressing in from all sides. The pink stops about 2 mm short of the hen's outline, so she sits in a thin border of bare paper. Headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in cut-paper capitals, black, over the pink.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: she's been waiting for us.

Picture: Night field, all bare paper with a thin black grass line near the bottom. Friend one, tall and thin with a long neck, kneels in the grass on the left, both hands lowering a black Toronto Maple Leafs toque (leaf logo drawn in outline) onto the head of the hen, who stands in front of him and looks straight at the reader. His dot eyes are wide open, mouth a small flat line, very solemn, like a coronation. Above them, filling the top half of the page, a flat pink spiral of eyes and interlocking triangles hangs in the sky and opens right over the hen. Nothing else on the page. The small text sits under the grass line, bottom left.

## Page 3
Words: none.

Picture: The farmhouse porch, drawn as three black steps and one post, lots of empty paper. Friend two, short and round in overalls and round black glasses, carries the hen in the toque with both arms held straight out, the way you carry a cake, toward a single wooden Muskoka chair on the right. Her face is calm and focused. The chair is black outline, but a flat pink sunburst of rays fans out behind it, and a pink rectangle floats over the seat like a cushion of light that is not there. The hen, carried, looks at the reader, unchanged.

Friend three, big and bald in a huge black puffer coat, stands in front of the chair with his back half to us, tipping an open paper sack of birdseed. The seed spiral is pink, each seed a flat pink dot, and the spiral grows more ornate at its edges into petals and eyes. His head tilts down, eyes closed, reverent. The hen looks at the reader. Small text at top right.

## Page 5

Picture: Bird's-eye view straight down on the grass. Friend four, with long black hair fanned out, lies curled on her side at the exact centre of the pink seed spiral, hands under her cheek, eyes open, a small peaceful smile, the only smile in the book so far. Nine plain white hens, black outline, no toques, stand on her and around her in a ring, all heads down, pecking. One stands on her hip. Around the ring, pink rays and pink eye shapes radiate out to the page edges, but the pink has holes wherever a hen is pecking. The toqued hen is not on this page. Small text along the bottom edge.
