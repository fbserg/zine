Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)
Picture: Square page, mostly bare paper. The title sits across the top third in bold cut-paper capitals, black. Low and centred, a small rubbery figure stands facing the reader, both hands flat under a white plate held at chest height. On the plate is a three-tier cake, taller than the figure's head, one candle. The figure wears a cone party hat. Black: the figure's outline, the plate, the cake's outline and tiers, the hat's outline and chin string, two dot eyes, no mouth. Orange: the frosting filling the cake shape, the candle flame, the hat. The orange layer sits 2 mm off the black, so the frosting already hangs over one side of the cake it belongs to. Nothing else on the page. No floor line.

## Page 2
Words: MONDAY (headline). Nobody asked for a cake. (small text)
Picture: A kitchen reduced to one black rectangle for the oven and one black horizontal line for the counter, both behind the figure. The figure stands dead centre, facing the reader, holding the finished cake out toward us at arm's length like a passport photo. Party hat on. Blank face, dot eyes, no mouth. Flour handprints on the front of the figure, drawn as flat black ovals. Black: figure, oven, counter, handprints, cake outline. Orange: frosting, candle flame, hat. Headline top left in cut-paper capitals, small typewriter line bottom left. The whole right half of the page is empty paper.

## Page 3
Words: TUESDAY (headline). The bus was full. (small text)
Picture: Bus interior, described by one vertical black pole and one black window rectangle and nothing more. The figure is jammed at the bottom of the frame, shoulders squashed up around the ears, holding the cake high over their head with both arms. A single large black elbow enters from the right edge and presses into the cake's top tier, denting it into a flat wedge. The figure's face stays blank, dot eyes level, hat crushed sideways. Black: pole, window, elbow, figure, cake outline with the dent. Orange: the frosting, still the full uncrushed cake shape, misregistered so it now bulges out past the dent into empty paper. Candle flame still lit. Paper above and left of the figure stays empty.

## Page 4
Words: WEDNESDAY (headline). Rain is good for frosting. (small text)
Picture: Flat rain drawn as evenly spaced straight black vertical lines across the whole page, nothing else of the city except one black kerb line at the bottom. The figure stands in the middle of the page, side on, walking left to right, cake held level in front of them. The party hat has folded over at the tip and hangs down in front of one eye. The frosting is sliding: orange runs down the sides of the black cake outline in three flat teardrops and puddles in a flat orange pool around the figure's shoes. The candle flame is gone, the black candle stub remains. Face blank, dot eyes, walking pace unhurried. Black: rain, kerb, figure, cake, candle. Orange: the running frosting, the pool, the drooping hat.

## Page 5
Words: THURSDAY (headline). BACK MONDAY (small text, on the note). I can wait. (small text)
Picture: A door as one tall black rectangle filling the right half of the page, with a black handle and a black doormat. A small square note is taped at head height, reading BACK MONDAY in small typewriter capitals. The figure stands with their back to us, facing the door, small against it, holding the cake at waist height. The cake is now a low black lump with one thin orange skin of frosting left on top, off register, so the orange sits mostly on the door beside the cake. Party hat still on, still folded over. We cannot see the face. The left half of the page is empty paper except for the headline top left and the typewriter line bottom left.

## Page 6
Words: FRIDAY (headline). A dog helped. (small text)
Picture: A kerb line runs across the lower third. The cake is face down on the pavement, drawn as a black lump with a flat orange splat spreading out from under it, misregistered so the splat runs away downhill toward the page edge. A medium black dog with dot eyes stands over it eating, rump toward us, tail flat and straight out. The figure sits on the kerb beside the dog, knees up, holding the now empty white plate level on their knees with both hands, as if still serving. Party hat on. Face blank, dot eyes, pointed at the dog. Black: kerb, dog, figure, plate outline, cake lump. Orange: the splat, the hat. Everything above the kerb is empty paper.

## Page 7
Words: SATURDAY (headline)
Picture: Night is not drawn. The page is empty off-white paper with the headline top left and a single small figure sitting alone at the bottom centre on the same black kerb line, knees up, empty plate flat on the knees. The party hat has collapsed to a soft fold. In one raised hand the figure holds the single black candle stub, lit, its small orange flame the only orange on the page, misregistered 2 mm so the flame floats just clear of the wick. Face blank, dot eyes, aimed straight ahead at nothing. No dog, no cake, no door, no rain. Four fifths of the page is paper.

## Page 8
Words: SUNDAY (headline). Next week, two. (small text)
Picture: Back to the Page 2 kitchen, same black oven rectangle, same counter line, same framing, so the reader lands on the repeat instantly. The figure stands dead centre facing us, cracking an egg over the counter with both hands. On the counter sit two mixing bowls, side by side, each holding a flat orange blob of batter. The party hat is back on, upright and crisp again, the only thing on the figure that has been repaired. Face blank, dot eyes, no mouth, exactly as on Page 2. Black: figure, oven, counter, bowls, eggshell. Orange: the two blobs of batter, the hat. Right half of the page empty paper. Headline top left, typewriter line bottom left.
