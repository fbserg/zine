Ink: Four flat gouache colours on cream card: black, warm red, dull teal, ochre. No fifth colour, no outline, black slightly off-register.

## Page 1
Words:
- UNPROMPTED (headline, hand-lettered rounded sans, lower left)

Picture: Cover. A short wide lighthouse keeper stands dead centre on cream paper, facing the reader, feet together, arms at his sides. He is a black coat and black boots with an ochre face and two black dot eyes, no mouth. His sweater is warm red. In his right hand, held out from his body at arm's length, is one lit match, a small ochre flame with a red core. Behind him, filling the upper right of the square and running off the top edge, is a dull teal lighthouse tower, flat and simple, its lamp room unlit and black. The teal tower prints a hair left of its black door, so the door sits half off the wall. Title in black, lower left, well clear of the figure. Everything else is cream.

## Page 2
Words:
- Bram keeps the light. (small text, upright, bottom centre)

Picture: Interior of the lamp room, seen straight on. The lamp fills two thirds of the frame: a big ochre drum of glass rings, flat concentric bands, sitting on a black base. It is blazing, so the cream paper around it is left bare to read as glare. Bram stands at the left edge of the frame on a black strip of floor, small against the lamp, reaching up with an oil can in his red-sleeved arm. His face is ochre, eyes two dots, mouth absent. A dull teal window frame crosses the top corners. His shadow is not drawn. Words in black at the bottom, small, with cream all around them.

## Page 3
Words:
- It reaches eleven miles. (small text, upright, bottom right)

Picture: Wide exterior night, but night is cream, not black. The lighthouse stands as a narrow teal column at the far left, cut off by the edge, its lamp room a solid ochre block. From it, one long flat ochre wedge sweeps right across the whole page and off the far edge, widening as it goes, laid over the cream. Under the wedge, a single black horizon line with three tiny black ship shapes on it, evenly spaced, the farthest one just touching the wedge's tip. No waves, no stars, no clouds. Bram is not on this page. Words in black under the horizon at the right.

## Page 4
Words:
- It does not reach the stairs. (small text, upright, top right)

Picture: The same tower, now cut open like a doll's house, standing tall and centred. The outer wall is dull teal. At the top, the lamp room is ochre and a short ochre wedge escapes sideways out of the frame. Everything below the lamp room is filled solid black: the whole spiral stair, drawn as a black column with thin cream steps spiralling down inside it, getting harder to see as they go lower, the bottom third of the tower pure black with no steps visible at all. Bram stands at the very top, a tiny figure on the lamp room floor, looking down into the black shaft, face ochre, eyes two dots, expression blank. Words small in black in the cream at the top right, beside the lamp room.

## Page 5
Words: (none)

Picture: Close on the stairs, seen from slightly above and behind. A steep black curve of steps runs from the upper left corner down to the lower right. Bram descends, shown small and three quarters from behind, red sweater, black coat, one black boot down on the next step. In his left hand he holds a stubby ochre candle with a small flame. The candle throws one flat pale ochre shape onto the two steps immediately around him, a soft rounded patch, and nothing beyond it. His own shadow is a large black shape thrown up the curved teal wall to his right, four times his size, with the same round head and the same blank profile. He is not looking at it. The rest of the page is cream, the stairs below him simply stopping where the black paint stops.

## Page 6
Words:
- Forty-one. Forty-two. (small text, upright, centre left)

Picture: Bram in the middle of the black stairwell, dead centre of the page, seen straight on, standing still with both boots on one step. The step is a short black bar under his feet. The candle in his hand is now a stub, the flame very small. The ochre light patch has shrunk to a circle that barely clears his shoulders and is drawn slightly off-register from him, so half his red sweater sits outside the light. His face is ochre, eyes two dots, and his mouth is a small black oval, open, mid-count. Above and below him, nothing but cream, no steps drawn at all, as though the staircase has stopped being there. Words in black to his left at eye height, small.

## Page 7
Words: (none)

Picture: The same framing, same centre, same height on the page, but the candle is out. Where the flame was there is a thin black curl of smoke rising two inches and stopping. The ochre light patch is gone, so the only ochre left on the page is Bram's face, a small flat shape in the middle of a page that is otherwise cream and black. His red sweater is still red. He has not moved: boots together, arms down, candle stub still held out. His eyes are the same two dots. Under his boots, one short black bar of step. No stairs above, no stairs below, no wall, no lighthouse. The most cream of any page in the book.

## Page 8
Words:
- He sleeps up here now. (headline, rounded sans, upper left)
- The ships do not mind. (small text, upright, bottom right)

Picture: Back in the lamp room. The big ochre lamp fills the right two thirds, blazing, bare cream around it for glare. On the left, on the black floor, a small mattress with a dull teal blanket, and Bram in it, flat on his back, tucked to the chin, head on an ochre pillow. His face is ochre and his two dot eyes are open, aimed straight up at nothing. One black boot sticks out from under the blanket at the foot end. Through the teal window frame behind the mattress, three tiny black ships sit on a black horizon line, in the same places as on page three. Headline in black at the upper left over cream, small text in black at the bottom right.
