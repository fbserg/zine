Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. A small upright vacuum cleaner stands, the same character on every page: rubbery rounded black-outlined body, short handle, two small wheels, a hose with a wide nozzle, two small dot eyes on the motor housing, no mouth, and a clear canister on its front with a small black crack at the very bottom. All dust in the book is flat orange. The vacuum stands alone in the centre of the square page on one thin black floor line. It faces the reader straight on, handle up, nozzle flat on the floor beside its left wheel. Its dot eyes look straight out, blank and content. The canister is exactly half full of orange dust. Under the canister, one small orange dot of dust sits on the floor, just below the black crack. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold black cut-paper capitals. The rest of the page is empty paper.

## Page 2
Words:
- Headline: HALF FULL.
- Small text: Good start.

Picture: The vacuum stands in profile, facing right, in the lower half of the page. Its body tilts back a little so its dot eyes look down at its own canister, which is exactly half full of orange dust. The pose is a quiet check, like a person who reads a gauge. The floor line is black and clean. HALF FULL. sits large in black above the vacuum. The small typewriter text Good start. sits under the floor line, bottom right. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: One more crumb.

Picture: The vacuum faces right, nozzle forward on the floor. One orange crumb flies up into the nozzle, with two short black motion lines. The vacuum's dot eyes look at the crumb, blank and focused. Directly under the canister, one orange crumb of the same size drops out of the black crack onto the floor behind the front wheel. The canister level stays exactly at the middle. The small typewriter text One more crumb. sits top left. Lots of empty paper.

## Page 4
Words:
- Headline: FEAST.

Picture: A tall orange pile of spilled flour fills the right side of the page, with a tipped-over black box beside it. The vacuum pushes its nozzle deep into the pile, body leaned forward with effort. Its dot eyes are wide apart and fixed on the pile. A thick orange stream pours out of the crack under the canister. Behind the vacuum, on the left, a second orange pile of the same size and shape grows on the floor. The canister is still exactly half full. FEAST. sits in big black cut-paper capitals at the top.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text: Still half full.

Picture: Close-up. The canister fills most of the page, drawn big with a thick black outline, orange dust exactly to the middle. The top edge of the frame shows the lower part of the motor housing and the two dot eyes, which look straight down at the canister. At the bottom of the frame, a small orange trickle falls from the black crack out of the picture. The small typewriter text Still half full. sits in the empty upper half of the canister, as if the vacuum reads it off the glass.

## Page 6
Words: none.

Picture: The vacuum drives from left to right across the lower third of the page. It is small, near the right edge, and cheerful in posture: handle straight up, nozzle forward. Behind it a long, thick orange trail of dust runs from the crack under the canister all the way back to the left edge of the page, with a few wiggles where it turned. The floor ahead of it, on the right, is clean paper. The canister is still exactly half full. No words.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text: Nearly clean.

Picture: View from directly above, looking down at a whole square room. Four black lines form the walls. The entire floor is covered in one long looping orange dust trail that crosses itself many times, like a scribble. In the middle of the scribble is the vacuum, drawn tiny from above: a black oval body, a black hose, two dot eyes that look up at the reader. One small square of clean paper remains in one corner. The small typewriter text Nearly clean. sits in that clean square.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: HALF FULL.
- Small text: Tomorrow: all the way.

Picture: The vacuum rests in a corner of the room, front view, cord coiled neatly around its handle. It sits in orange dust up to the tops of its wheels. The dust spreads flat across the whole floor to both edges of the page. The canister is still exactly half full. Its dot eyes look straight at the reader, blank and satisfied. HALF FULL. sits in big black cut-paper capitals on the wall above it. The small typewriter text Tomorrow: all the way. sits at the bottom, printed in black over the orange dust.
