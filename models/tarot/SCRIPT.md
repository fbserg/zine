Ink: fluorescent pink

Every page is one tarot card. A thick black border runs around the square page with a margin of paper outside it. A black band across the bottom of the card holds the headline, reversed out in paper-white cut-paper capitals. The Roman numeral sits small at top centre, inside the border. The day line sits in small typewriter just above the bottom band. Each composition is centred and near-symmetrical, one figure on a clear axis, with empty paper as the ground.

The character on every page: a short woman with a round head, a blunt black bob with a straight fringe, small dot eyes, no mouth or a flat line mouth. She wears a plain paper-white smock dress with a black round collar and pink socks with black shoes. Her face never changes: blank, patient.

The setting from page 2 on: the inside of a whale's stomach. The walls and floor are flat fluorescent pink. The ribs are thick black arches that curve up either side of the card, like the pillars of a temple. Everything she owns came in with her.

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: The card has no numeral. The top half is empty paper sky with a small pink sun dead centre. A thin black horizon line crosses the middle. On the line, exactly centred, she sits in a tiny black rowboat and reads a paperback, eyes down, calm. Directly below her, filling the whole lower half of the card, a huge black whale rises from underneath, seen head-on, its mouth wide open in a perfect symmetrical oval. The inside of the mouth is solid fluorescent pink, with a row of black baleen teeth along the top edge. The boat sits exactly on the rim of the mouth, one second from going in. She has not noticed. The UNPROMPTED headline sits in the black bottom band.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text (top): I
- Headline: THE RUG
- Small text: Monday. Straight.

Picture: Inside the whale. Two black ribs arch up the left and right edges of the card and nearly meet at the top, framing her like a throne arch. The pink floor curves gently like a bowl. She kneels dead centre on a rectangular rug with black fringe at both ends and a black diamond pattern, her two hands pressed flat on its front edge, nudging it. The rug is perfectly square to the card border. The pink floor under it is wet and wavy with a few black drip lines. Her face is blank and serious. Her small black suitcase stands closed, upright, in the lower left corner, like a card symbol.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text (top): II
- Headline: THE FERN
- Small text: Tuesday. Water it anyway.

Picture: She stands dead centre, facing us, holding a small watering can tipped over a potted fern on the pink floor in front of her. Water falls from the can in short black dash lines. From the top of the card, the whale's own fluid drips down on everything in long pink and black drops: on her head, on the fern, on the can. The fern is already dripping. Her bob is wet and flat. Her expression does not change. The ribs frame her left and right like the two pillars of the High Priestess, one filled black and one outlined in pink.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text (top): III
- Headline: THE POST
- Small text: Wednesday.
- Small text (hand-lettered on the letter, typewriter style): Dear Mum, fine.

Picture: She stands in the centre, one arm raised straight up like the Magician, holding a clear glass bottle with a black cork. Her other hand holds a sheet of paper open at chest height, turned to face us, and it reads "Dear Mum, fine." in small typewriter letters. Above her, the whale's throat is a black tunnel narrowing to a small dark circle at top centre, with pink ring shapes around it. Three more corked bottles already lie on the pink floor at her feet, rolled back down. She does not look at them.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text (top): IV
- Headline: THE FRINGE
- Small text: Thursday. Shorter.

Picture: She kneels in profile at the centre over a round pink puddle on the floor, which reflects her head upside down in black. She holds a large pair of black scissors open at her forehead. The fringe on her real head is now cut in a steep diagonal, very short on one side. The reflection shows the same crooked fringe. A small pile of black hair lies on the pink floor beside her knee. Her face in profile is completely calm. The ribs arch over her like a crown.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text (top): V
- Headline: THE GUEST
- Small text: Friday. Two cups.

Picture: A small round table stands dead centre with a black tablecloth. She sits on a stool on the left, facing right, pouring tea from a black teapot into a pink cup. On a stool on the right sits a small grey-black fish, upright on its tail, one dot eye facing us, water dripping from it in pink drops. A second pink cup sits in front of the fish, already full. The two figures mirror each other like the two seated figures of a tarot court. Neither face shows anything. Behind them, one black rib arches over the whole table.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text (top): VI
- Headline: THE BATHER
- Small text: Saturday. Maybe not.

Picture: She stands dead centre at the edge of a round pool that fills the lower third of the card. The pool is solid fluorescent pink with black bubble circles rising from it and a few thin black steam lines. She wears a black one-piece swimsuit, a black rubber swim cap over her bob, and goggles pushed up on the cap. Her arms are held out straight in front of her, ready to dive. One foot hovers above the pool. Her face is blank. A towel lies folded neatly on the floor behind her, and a black sign on a stick stands at the pool edge with no words on it, only a skull drawn in pink.

## Page 8
Words:
- Small text (top): VII
- Headline: THE WORLD
- Small text: Sunday. Staying in.

Picture: The whale's mouth is open wide, and the view is from inside, looking out. The mouth is a symmetrical oval frame of black teeth filling the card, like the wreath on the World card. Through it, outside, is empty paper sky, a pink sun low on a black horizon, a small beach, and a wooden black pier that runs right up to the whale's lip, close enough to step onto. In the centre of the mouth, she sits in a black armchair with her back to the pier, her rug under the chair and her fern beside it. She reaches up with one hand and pulls a pink curtain across the opening, already half closed over the sun. Her face turns to us, blank and content.
