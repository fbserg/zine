Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: A wooden fence runs across the lower third of the square page, black outlines on bare paper. Gerald, a round rubbery man in a small brimmed hat, leans both forearms on the fence and faces right, toward an empty yard. His dot eyes are blank and his mouth is a short flat line. Two mugs stand on the fence post beside his elbow. Both mugs are solid orange. One mug has three wavy black steam lines, the other has none. His hat band is orange. A flat orange sun disc sits high in the upper right. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: Every morning at seven, Gerald goes out to the fence.

Picture: The top third is a silent-film intertitle card: a solid black rectangle with a thin orange border, and the small typewriter text in paper-white letters inside it. Below the card, a wide flat view shows two identical small houses side by side, with one fence between their back yards. On the left, Gerald steps out of his open back door with an orange mug in each hand, eyes forward, face blank. His lawn is neat. The house on the right has its curtains shut, and the curtains are solid orange. Its grass is long, drawn as tall black tufts. A folding lawn chair stands empty in that yard. A brimmed hat with an orange band hangs on a hook beside the right-hand back door.

## Page 3
Words:
- Headline: MORNING, BRIAN!
- Small text: Brian has not come out since March.

Picture: Gerald stands on his side of the fence, one hand raised in a friendly wave, the other hand holding out an orange mug over the fence toward the right. His face is blank, mouth a small open circle. On the right side of the fence: the empty lawn chair, the long black grass, the orange curtains shut tight, the hat on its hook. A single black leaf lies on the chair seat. The intertitle card with MORNING, BRIAN! in paper-white cut-paper capitals sits in the upper left, black with an orange border. The small typewriter line sits in black directly under the card, on bare paper.

## Page 4
Words:
- Headline: MORNING, GERALD!

Picture: The view flips. Gerald is now on Brian's side of the fence, sitting in Brian's lawn chair, wearing Brian's hat with its orange band. He holds the second orange mug in both hands. He faces left toward his own empty yard. Gerald's own hat sits alone on top of the fence post, orange band toward the reader. The hook by Brian's door is empty. Gerald's expression is exactly the same blank dot-eyed face as before. The intertitle card with MORNING, GERALD! in paper-white capitals sits in the upper right, black with an orange border. The rest is paper.

## Page 5
Words:
- Headline: LOOKS LIKE RAIN.

Picture: Gerald is back on his own side, wearing his own hat, and he looks up at the sky with his head tilted back and his dot eyes turned up. One small flat black cloud sits in the upper left and partly covers the orange sun disc. On the other side of the fence, Brian's hat rests on the empty lawn chair. Black dashed motion lines arc over the top of the fence from right to left, to show that Gerald just climbed back. The intertitle card with LOOKS LIKE RAIN. sits at the top center, black with an orange border.

## Page 6
Words:
- Headline: COULD DO WITH IT.

Picture: Gerald is halfway through climbing back to Brian's side. One leg is still hooked over the top rail of the fence, the other foot reaches for Brian's grass. Brian's hat sits crooked on his head, and his own hat has fallen onto his side of the lawn. He looks straight out at the reader, face blank, one hand raised in a thoughtful palm-up gesture as he delivers the reply. The small black cloud now fully covers the orange sun. Two orange sweat drops fly off his forehead. The intertitle card with COULD DO WITH IT. sits in the upper right, black with an orange border.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text: This goes on until lunch.

Picture: Gerald lies flat on his stomach along the top rail of the fence, arms dangling down on his side and legs dangling down on Brian's side, like a towel on a line. Both hats are stacked on his head, his own on top. His dot eyes stare at nothing. Both orange mugs lie on their sides in the grass below, empty. A clock on the wall of Gerald's house shows both black hands pointing straight up at twelve, and its face is orange. The orange sun sits directly overhead, centered at the top of the page. The small typewriter line sits in black at the bottom right on bare paper, no card.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: SAME TIME TOMORROW?

Picture: Gerald stands in his open back door on the left, half turned to look back over his shoulder at Brian's house, one hand on the door frame. He wears his own hat. He carries both orange mugs in the other hand. Brian's hat hangs back on its hook, and the lawn chair is empty again. In Brian's window on the right, one orange curtain is pulled back a narrow crack, and in that black gap sits a single white dot eye, looking at Gerald. Nothing else about the house changes. The intertitle card with SAME TIME TOMORROW? sits at the top center, black with an orange border. The rest of the page is empty paper.
