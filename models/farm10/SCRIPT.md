Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Mostly empty paper. Top edge: UNPROMPTED in black cut-paper capitals. Centre: one wooden fence post, flat black, with a raccoon sitting upright on top. The raccoon is black line and black mask, small dot eyes, paws held together at its belly, face blank, looking straight at the reader. Behind its head sits one large flat fluorescent pink circle, misregistered off to the right, so it reads as a moon and as a halo at the same time. No ground, no sky, no other marks.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: Oh. It's you.

Picture: TESS, the first friend: short black bob, a huge puffy black parka, rubber boots. She lies on her back across the bottom third of the page, in a flat black strip of grass. A small glass pipe rests on her chest with one thin pink wisp of smoke. The top two thirds is not sky. It is a flat fluorescent pink pattern of nested diamonds that opens in the middle like a door. The raccoon stands on its hind legs beside her head. Tess reaches up with both arms and pulls her own black toque down onto the raccoon's head. The toque has a small pink maple leaf on the front. Tess's face is calm, dot eyes wide. The raccoon's face stays blank. The small text sits in the empty grass near her mouth.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: Yes. I understand.

Picture: OWEN, the second friend: tall, thin, black beard, long arms, plaid shirt drawn as flat black stripes. Inside a barn, drawn as a plain black doorway shape with paper inside. The raccoon, still in the toque, sits upright on one hay bale in the centre. Behind it, fluorescent pink arches rise in rows like a cathedral, thin pink rays fan out from the toque. Owen kneels on the floor in the foreground, seen from behind and to the side. He holds a white cooler out toward the raccoon with the lid swung open, his head bowed, forehead almost on the lid. The raccoon looks past him at nothing. The small text sits under Owen, near the floor.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: Rest now.

Picture: DEV, the third friend: round glasses, bucket hat, short and wide. One object fills the page: the white cooler on the ground, seen from the side. The raccoon sits inside the cooler among black hot dog packs and cans. Only its masked face and the toque show above the rim. The inside of the cooler glows flat fluorescent pink, and two straight pink beams go up from it like an ark in a painting. Dev stands behind the cooler, bent at the waist, both hands flat on the lid. He lowers the lid over the raccoon with great care. His face is solemn, glasses blank circles. The raccoon's face stays blank. The small text sits low on the empty paper to the left.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text: Go home.

Picture: PRIYA, the fourth friend: one long black braid, overalls, bare feet. A pond fills the lower half as a flat fluorescent pink shape. Inside the pink sit small black stars and pink rings, so the pond reads as outer space. Priya crouches at the black edge of the pond, arms stretched out, fingertips on the back of the closed white cooler. She pushes it off onto the water like a funeral boat. A striped black raccoon tail sticks out from under the lid. Priya's face is serene, dot eyes half shut. Top half of the page is empty paper. The small text sits in the empty space above her head.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: Take me with you.

Picture: SAM, the fifth friend: small, shaved head, oversized black hoodie. The white cooler floats in the centre of the pond, lid pushed open. The raccoon sits on the open lid in the toque, both paws holding a hot dog, eating. Around the cooler, huge flat fluorescent pink rings ripple out to the edges of the page. Sam stands in the water in the foreground, black water line at his chest, hoodie floating up. Both his arms reach up toward the raccoon. His face is blank and certain. The raccoon does not look at him. The small text floats on the paper above Sam's hands.

## Page 7

Picture: No pink on this page at all. Black ink only, the pink drum does not print. Plain farm at night, drawn thin and flat: one fence line, one silo, one parked pickup truck, a strip of grass. The raccoon walks across the bottom of the page from left to right, on all fours, wet and dripping black drops. It wears the toque and drags a whole pack of hot dogs in its mouth. Its face is blank. Far behind it, small in the middle ground, five dark heads stick up out of the pond, all facing the wrong way.
