Ink: blue

Blue rule for the whole book: blue marks only what belongs to the missing second person (the flat silhouette, the second of each pair, the spare key) plus the cover box and the final tick. Everything else is black. The character: a rubbery bald figure in a black roll-neck sweater, small dot eyes, a straight line for a mouth, one hair sticking up from the top of the head. Same figure on every page. Every page sits in a thin black rule box like an IKEA manual page, with wide empty paper.
