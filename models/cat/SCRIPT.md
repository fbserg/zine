Ink: green

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold cut-paper capitals, black. Below it, centred on generous empty paper, sits one cat, facing the reader, upright, front paws together, tail curled around its feet. The cat is paper-white with a thick black outline, one black patch on its back, a black nose, and small black dot eyes. Its face is blank. A flat green gift bow sits stuck to the top of its head, slightly crooked. The bow is the only green on the page. Nothing else is in the frame.

## Page 2
Words:
- Headline: MONDAY
- Small text: A mouse.

Picture: This page sets the frame for pages 2 to 8. A closed bedroom door stands in the middle of the page, drawn as a black outline with a round black doorknob and a thin black gap under it. A single black floor line runs across the page at the foot of the door. The cat sits on the floor to the left of the door in its cover pose, facing the reader, blank dot eyes. On the floor to the right of the door, neatly placed, lies a small flat green mouse on its side, tail straight. The cat does not look at the mouse. MONDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "A mouse." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 3
Words:
- Headline: TUESDAY
- Small text: A sock.

Picture: Same frame as page 2: the closed door, the black floor line, the cat seated left in the identical pose with the identical blank face. To the right of the door lies one long flat green sock, laid out straight and tidy, as if on display. TUESDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "A sock." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 4
Words:
- Headline: WEDNESDAY
- Small text: A boot. Not ours.

Picture: Same frame, same door, same cat, same pose, same blank face. To the right of the door stands one enormous flat green work boot, upright, laces trailing. The boot is taller than the cat and much too big for anyone in the house. WEDNESDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "A boot. Not ours." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 5
Words:
- Headline: THURSDAY
- Small text: The neighbour's gnome.

Picture: Same frame, same door, same cat, same pose, same blank face. To the right of the door stands a garden gnome as tall as the doorknob, flat green all over, with a black outline, a pointed hat, a beard, and small black dot eyes that stare at the reader exactly like the cat. A small clump of black soil clings to the base of the gnome. THURSDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "The neighbour's gnome." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 6
Words:
- Headline: FRIDAY
- Small text: A raccoon.

Picture: Same frame, same door, same cat, same pose, same blank face. To the right of the door stands a Toronto green bin, the square wheeled compost bin, flat green with a black outline, its lid propped open a crack. A raccoon peers out through the gap: paper-white face, black mask, black dot eyes, two small black paws on the rim. The raccoon's face is as blank as the cat's. The bin is the only green on the page. FRIDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "A raccoon." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 7
Words:
- Headline: SATURDAY
- Small text: The car. Ours, at least.

Picture: Same frame, but a whole small hatchback car now fills the hallway to the right of the door, flat green with a black outline, black tyres, jammed wall to wall. Its front bumper touches the door frame and its rear runs off the right edge of the page. The cat no longer sits on the floor. It sits on the roof of the car in the identical upright pose, tail curled, facing the reader with the same blank face. The door is still closed. SATURDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "The car. Ours, at least." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: MONDAY
- Small text: A mouse.

Picture: Same frame as page 2, the car gone, the closed door, the black floor line. The positions swap. The raccoon now sits on the left of the door in the cat's exact pose: upright, paws together, tail curled, black mask, blank dot eyes on the reader. The cat sits on the right of the door, in the spot where the gifts used to lie. A small flat green mouse lies on the floor at the cat's front paws. For the first time, the cat looks down at the gift instead of at the reader. Its face stays blank. MONDAY sits top left in cut-paper capitals. "A mouse." sits bottom right in small typewriter text.
