Ink: fluorescent pink

Black is the farm as it actually is. Fluorescent pink is charge: what the fence is holding and what Rowan is seeing. The 2 mm misregistration is never corrected, so every pink thing sits slightly off its black thing, and by page 5 that gap is the whole joke.

## Page 1

Words: UNPROMPTED (headline, bold cut-paper capitals, lower left, sitting on the horizon line)

Picture: Cover. A square of empty off-white paper that is mostly field. The horizon is a single thick black line high up, with three black fence posts along it, small, evenly spaced, going off to the right. One fluorescent pink wire runs dead straight from edge to edge at about neck height on the figure, unbroken, the only pink on the page apart from a small pink halo of grain along the wire. Rowan is a small black figure in the lower right third, rubbery and simplified, walking left to right in a flat side view, thick even outline, one dot eye visible, blank. Both arms hang. Her face is tilted up and away from the wire, toward the sky, which is blank paper. She is about four steps from the wire. Nothing else is on the page. The title sits in the empty field below her, not touching her.

## Page 2

Words: Nobody wandered off. That was the rule. (small typewriter, single line, bottom edge, centred)

Picture: Five black figures stand in a loose row across the middle of the page, all the same rubbery build, all flat front view, all with two small dot eyes and no mouths. They are not touching. Each one faces a slightly different direction, feet pointing five different ways, which is the only thing that moves in the drawing. Rowan is second from the right, identifiable by a black bowl of hair that sits on her head like a helmet. Under and behind the row, in fluorescent pink and offset 2 mm down and left, is a second row of the same five figures, identical outlines, so each person has a pink copy standing a little out of step with them. Pure paper above and below. No horizon, no grass, no moon.

## Page 3

Words: none

Picture: Full page, low angle. Six goats stand in a perfect circle facing inward, drawn in flat black silhouette, boxy bodies, straight legs, rectangular pupils drawn as small black slots. The circle is empty in the middle. In fluorescent pink, offset, each goat has a second outline and a single pink beam running from its eye into the empty centre, so six pink lines meet at one point on bare paper. Rowan's head and shoulders enter from the bottom edge of the page, back of the head to us, black, the bowl of hair unmistakable. She is perfectly still. The rest of the page is paper.

## Page 4

Words: back in a second (small typewriter, tiny, floating just above Rowan's head)

Picture: The widest page in the book, seen from far away. Rowan is a small black figure in the lower left, mid stride, walking right, one arm lifted in a flat wave behind her without looking back. Her expression is one dot eye, blank. Off at the far right edge, four black figures stand in a clump, tiny, watching nothing. Crossing the entire page horizontally at Rowan's neck height is the fluorescent pink wire, thin, dead straight, with one black fence post and one small black ceramic insulator drawn where the wire meets it, about a third of the way in. The wire passes in front of Rowan by the 2 mm offset, so it is visibly ahead of her. Two short pink ticks of grain sit on the wire like it is humming. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 5

Words: none

Picture: Full bleed on the figure, no horizon, no ground. Rowan is large and centred, black, drawn rigid: legs straight and pressed together, both arms out from the body at forty-five degrees, fingers spread flat, bowl of hair lifted clean off her head and hovering as a separate black shape two centimetres above her scalp. Both dot eyes are the same size as before, blank, unchanged. The fluorescent pink wire runs straight across her throat, edge to edge. Around her, in flat pink, a burst of eight straight spikes radiating outward like a child's sun, none of them touching her outline. Her pink offset copy is the only misregistration that matters here: it sits 2 mm off, so she looks like she is in two places. No words, no sound effect, nothing else on the paper.

## Page 6

Words: none

Picture: Same flat side view and same walking pose as page 4, same size, same distance, as if page 5 never happened and this is the next frame of a loop. Rowan walks right, one arm mid swing, one dot eye, blank. The only differences: her bowl of hair now stands straight up in a single black spike, one shoe is gone and the bare foot is drawn with three small toes, and a thin pink curl of smoke rises off the spike of hair. Behind her, the pink wire is still there but now has one small V shaped sag in it at exactly her height. The rest is paper. She is not looking at any of it.

## Page 7

Words: Did something happen? (small typewriter, above the friend) / No. (small typewriter, above Rowan)

Picture: The five of them again, a row across the middle, flat front view, same as page 2 so the reader lands on the comparison without being told. Rowan is second from the right, hair a vertical black spike, one foot bare, smoke gone. The four friends have their dot eyes drawn shifted hard to one side, all looking at her hair, which is the only eye movement in the book. Rowan's two dot eyes look straight out at the reader, dead centre, blank. In fluorescent pink and offset, every one of the five figures now has their hair standing up in the pink layer, including the four who never touched the fence. Empty paper above and below the row.

## Page 8

Words: In the morning the fence was still telling everyone. (small typewriter, single line, bottom edge, centred)

Picture: Back cover. Morning, so the page is mostly bare paper with no dark mass anywhere. A single black fence post stands right of centre with its small black ceramic insulator. The wire runs edge to edge, in black this time, with one small V shaped sag in it. Caught on the wire at the sag is a single black tuft of hair, four strands, drawn like a tiny brush. In fluorescent pink, offset, three short flat rays come off the sag, and far away on the horizon line five very small pink figures walk away to the left in single file, no black copies underneath them. Nothing else. No logo, no credits, no number.
