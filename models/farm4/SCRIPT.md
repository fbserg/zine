Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Cover. Dead centre of the square page, one figure stands facing the reader: rubbery simplified body, overalls, bucket hat, two small dot eyes, no mouth, blank. She holds a long fence post upright in one hand like a flagpole and a mallet in the other. A goat stands balanced on the flat top of the post, level with her hat, calm, dot eyes, facing the same direction she does. Everything else is bare paper. The figure, the post, the mallet and all outlines are black. The goat's body is a flat fluorescent orange shape sitting inside its black outline and slipping 2 mm off it, and the same orange fills the word UNPROMPTED in bold cut-paper capitals across the top quarter of the page. No ground line, no horizon, no sky.

## Page 2
Words: Six goats. One fence. (small text)

Picture: Wide and low. A single flat black horizon line across the lower third, nothing on it but grass ticks. The figure kneels at the left, mallet raised behind her head, driving the first post in. Her expression is blank. Six goats stand in a loose row to the right of her, evenly spaced, all in profile, all facing her, doing nothing: flat orange bodies with black outlines and black legs, the orange shifted 2 mm left of the drawing. The small typewriter line sits alone in the empty paper of the top left corner. Two thirds of the page is empty sky.

## Page 3
Words: none

Picture: Same low horizon. Four black posts now stand upright across the page with two straight wire lines strung between them, clean and taut, dividing the frame into near side and far side. The figure stands at the far right, hands at her sides, looking along the fence, blank. All six goats are on the far side of the wire, small, in a tidy clump, orange bodies offset 2 mm. Nothing else in the frame. Paper above, paper below.

## Page 4
Words: none

Picture: The identical frame as the previous page, copied: same four posts, same two wires, same horizon, no gap anywhere in the fence, nothing broken. The figure has turned her head to the reader, dot eyes, blank. All six goats are now on her side, in the same tidy clump, same spacing, orange offset 2 mm, facing her. The far side is empty paper.

## Page 5
Words: Taller. (small text)

Picture: Tall vertical composition. One post fills the centre of the page from the bottom edge to the top third, with three rails nailed across it, the fence now twice the earlier height, all black. One goat stands on the topmost rail, four hooves together on the narrow board, body flat orange, black outline offset, perfectly still, looking down. The figure stands at the bottom left, tiny by comparison, arms at her sides, head tipped back, blank. The small typewriter word sits at the bottom right in empty paper.

## Page 6
Words: Lunch. (small text)

Picture: The figure sits on the ground at the left, legs straight out, holding a sandwich in both hands halfway to where a mouth would be. She does not look up. All six goats stand pressed in a tight arc around her, close enough to touch, heads level with her head, dot eyes on the sandwich, orange bodies with black outlines slipping 2 mm. Behind the group the tall fence runs off the right edge, black, empty on its far side. The whole top half of the page is bare paper. The word sits small under her boots.

## Page 7
Words: Done. (small text)

Picture: Night. A single black fence line runs the full width of the page at mid height, posts and three rails, straight and finished, both ends leaving the frame. The figure stands at the centre in front of it, a mallet hanging from one hand, shoulders down, blank, facing the reader. No goats in the frame at all. A flat orange disc of moon sits high in the empty paper at the top right, its black outline circle offset 2 mm from it. The word sits small at the bottom centre.

## Page 8
Words: SIX GOATS. ONE FENCE. (headline)

Picture: Morning, pulled far back, the whole farm seen from above and slightly askew. The finished fence is a small closed square in the middle of the page, black, no gate. The figure stands alone inside it, the size of a thumbnail, arms at her sides, dot eyes up at the reader, blank. All six goats stand outside the square, spaced evenly around it, all facing in, flat orange bodies with black outlines offset 2 mm. Beyond them the page is empty off-white paper to every edge, no barn, no road, no trees. The headline runs across the bottom edge in bold cut-paper capitals, printed in orange.
