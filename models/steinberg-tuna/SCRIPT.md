## Page 1

- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: One figure on empty off-white paper. A single thin black horizontal line crosses the lower third of the page like a drawn floor, and MR. HOLLIS stands on it, centred, small against the white. He wears a black suit, a black tie and a black bowler hat. His head is a paper grocery bag, left paper-white with a thick black outline. On the bag, a man's face is drawn in thin wobbly black line: dot eyes, a straight moustache, a flat mouth. Under the hat brim, the bag has two small pointed bumps, as if something pushes up from inside. From under the back hem of his jacket hangs a long fluorescent orange tail with black stripes, curled at the tip into a question mark. His arms hang straight. He faces the reader, blank. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold cut-paper black capitals, the letters slightly uneven. The orange layer sits about 2 mm off the black, so the tail floats a little away from its outline.
