Ink: fluorescent orange

Recurring character, every page: a stout woman in her fifties. Short black bob, small dot eyes, flat mouth, no expression ever. She wears a belted trench coat (paper colour, thick black outline) over a flowered housedress, with fuzzy slippers. Orange is always light: lamps, porch lights, the stripes through blinds, the oven glow. Black is always shadow.

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. A night street seen from low down, a single streetlamp at top right. The lamp throws a tall orange cone down onto the pavement. The rest of the page is solid black except the paper-white edge of the kerb. The woman stands at the bottom of the cone, small in the frame, feet together, trench coat belted tight. She holds one quilted oven mitt raised at chest height like a detective holding up evidence. Her face is blank. Her long black shadow runs out of the cone and off the left edge of the page. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in cut-paper capitals, knocked out of the black in paper white.

## Page 2
Words:
- small text: It was my mother's dish.

Picture: Her kitchen at night. Venetian blind light falls across the whole page as diagonal orange stripes over solid black. A kitchen cupboard stands open at centre. On the middle shelf, among dust drawn as fine black specks, sits one clean paper-white rectangle where a dish used to be. The woman stands at the left edge, cut off at the shoulder by the frame, one finger pointed at the clean rectangle. An orange stripe crosses her eyes like a mask. Her face is blank. The small text sits in the bottom right corner of paper.

## Page 3
Words:
- small text: Last seen at her funeral lunch.

Picture: A church basement from a high, steep overhead angle, like a camera in the ceiling. A long folding table runs diagonally corner to corner. On it sit twenty identical rectangular casserole dishes in rows, all black outline on paper, all the same. One dish in the middle is empty and filled flat orange. A single bare bulb at the top makes an orange pool on the table. Everything outside the pool is black. The woman stands at the far end of the table, tiny, a paper-white shape at the edge of the light, looking down the row. Her face is blank.

## Page 4
Words:
- small text: Pyrex. Cornflowers. Glass lid. Talk.

Picture: Interrogation. A small old church lady with a round perm sits on a folding chair at centre, holding a paper plate with a wobbling cube of jelly on it. A hanging lamp directly above her throws a hard orange cone onto her and the plate. The page around the cone is solid black. The woman leans in from the right, only her trench coat shoulder, one hand flat on the table, and her blank dot-eyed face entering the light. The church lady stares straight out at the reader, blank, a fork halfway to her mouth. The small text sits in paper white against the black at the top left.

## Page 5
Words:
- small text: Nobody ever remembers taking a dish home.

Picture: A police lineup. A back wall with black height lines and paper stripes. Five casserole dishes stand on their ends in a row against the wall, like suspects, each a different shape: oval, square, round, a loaf tin, a tall crock. A flat orange spotlight band lights the wall behind them. In the foreground, dark and cut off at the bottom of the frame, the woman is a black silhouette from behind, hand on her chin, only the outline of her bob and the belt of the trench coat showing. The small text runs along the bottom in paper white on her black silhouette.

## Page 6
Words:
- small text: Then Gary's porch light came on.

Picture: A stakeout. The woman crouches behind a hedge in the bottom left foreground, the hedge solid black, only her bob and dot eyes over the top. She holds a pair of binoculars that are just two black circles. Across a dark lawn, far back and small at upper right, a house is solid black except one porch light, a flat orange cone falling down onto the front steps. Most of the page is black and empty. Her face is blank. The small text sits at top left in paper white.

## Page 7
Words:
- small text: Heard you weren't eating.

Picture: A front door seen from just behind the woman's shoulder, low angle. The door stands open. Orange hall light floods out in a tall flat rectangle and falls across the doormat and over her. Gary fills the doorway: a large soft man in a cardigan and big oven mitts, eyebrows as two flat lines, face blank. He holds out her mother's dish, full, covered in crinkled foil drawn as a few black lines. The dish has little cornflowers drawn in black on its side. The woman's fist is still raised in the air, mid-knock, frozen. The small text sits in the black at the bottom, paper white.

## Page 8
Words:
- headline: CASE CLOSED.
- small text: Her recipe. Too much paprika.

Picture: Her kitchen at night again, same blind stripes as page 2, orange diagonals over black. The woman sits alone at a small table at centre, still in the trench coat and slippers, eating straight from the cornflower dish with a fork. The dish is full of a flat orange casserole. The glass lid lies beside it with an index card taped to it, the card showing only a few squiggle lines, no letters. On the shelf behind her, the clean rectangle in the dust is still empty. Her face is blank, fork in her mouth. The headline CASE CLOSED. sits at the top in cut-paper capitals. The small text sits below the table on paper.
