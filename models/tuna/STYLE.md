Black and white photocopy, 1990s copy-shop zine. Cut-and-paste collage on white paper: hand-cut edges with shadows, ransom-note letters cut from magazines for headlines, word-processor Courier strips for small text, Sharpie marker drawing, clip-art and copied product photos gone to halftone, toner fade and a dark copier edge, high contrast, greys only as halftone. Deadpan drawing: simplified figures in marker, blank expressions, plenty of white. Square page. Every letter legible and spelled exactly.
