Ink: metallic gold

Recurring character, same on every page: a round-bellied man of about sixty. He is bald on top, with long straight grey hair down his back and a grey beard. He wears a black bathrobe, open over a black T-shirt, plus fuzzy slippers and one studded leather wristband. He has small dot eyes and a flat, blank mouth on every page, whatever he does.

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover composed like a 1980s metal album. The man stands in the dead centre of the frame on a suburban curb, legs wide, feet planted. He holds a closed cardboard box high over his head in both hands, like a warrior with a sword raised to the sky. His bathrobe flies out behind him like a cape. Blank face, dot eyes. Behind him, a huge gold sunburst fills the top half of the page, with jagged gold lightning bolts that hit the box. At his feet, a small black recycling bin sits on the curb. A plain black suburban house outline sits low on the horizon. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold cut-paper capitals, black with a gold offset edge, arched like a metal band logo with pointed ends on the U and the D.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: Recycling day.
- Small text (stencilled on boxes): MERCH
- Small text (stencilled on boxes): TOUR 1988
- Small text (stencilled on boxes): FLYERS

Picture: Composed like a warrior at the peak of a mountain of skulls. The open garage door frames a tall pyramid of cardboard boxes, drawn in black outline. The man stands at the very top of the pile, one slipper forward, and holds a coffee mug up in the air like a trophy. Blank face. Three boxes near the front show the stencilled words MERCH, TOUR 1988 and FLYERS. Behind the pile, a flat gold full moon fills the back wall of the garage. Gold cobwebs hang in the top corners. The words "Recycling day." sit small in typewriter at the bottom left, on empty paper.

## Page 3
Words:
- Headline (printed on the shirt): GRAVE TIDE
- Small text: 4,000 shirts. World tour 1988. Cancelled.

Picture: Composed like a battle banner cover. The man stands knee-deep in an open box in the middle of the driveway. He holds one giant XL T-shirt stretched high between both fists, like a war flag in a storm. The shirt is black with the band logo in gold: a winged skull on a breaking wave, with GRAVE TIDE above it in spiky metal capitals. Wind pulls the shirt and his long hair to the right. More identical folded shirts spill out of the box around his legs. His face stays blank. Plain paper sky. The small text sits under the box in typewriter.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: The last boxes.

Picture: Composed like a chained barbarian dragging a war chariot. The man leans far forward, almost horizontal, and pulls a rope over his shoulder. The rope ties to a small squeaky hand trolley with a tower of six boxes on it. The trolley has tiny wheels. He walks down a long driveway that runs from the top right corner to the bottom left. A huge gold setting sun sits low behind him, and his black silhouette overlaps it. Gold dust puffs rise from his slippers. Blank face, dot eyes, looking straight ahead. The small text sits in the empty top left corner.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text (sticker on the bin): FLATTEN ALL BOXES

Picture: Composed like a hero standing on a slain dragon. The man stands on top of the overflowing recycling bin, one slipper on the heaped boxes, one fist on his hip, and the other arm pointing at the horizon. The flattened boxes under him bend and crack like the scales of a beast. A white sticker on the front of the bin reads FLATTEN ALL BOXES in typewriter. The bin is solid gold. Black flames of torn cardboard shoot out of the bin around his ankles. Blank face, dot eyes, calm. Empty paper all around.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: 7:02 AM

Picture: Composed like a knight facing a monster. A garbage truck comes around the corner at the right edge of the page, drawn huge and low, like a beast with its jaws open. Its two headlights are gold eyes and gold light pours out of the back hopper like fire breath. Its robot arm lifts the gold bin high like a claw. The man stands small on the far left curb, feet together, and gives a single devil horns salute with one hand. His other hand holds the coffee mug. His bathrobe blows back in the truck's wind. Blank face. The time sits small in typewriter in the top left.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text (label on the cassette): DEMO

Picture: Composed like a sacred relic cover. The man stands alone on the empty curb, facing us, with the empty bin lying on its side beside him. He holds one black cassette tape up in front of his chest in both hands, like a holy stone. A white label on the cassette reads DEMO in handwritten capitals. Gold rays shine out from the cassette in a perfect circle and cover most of the page. His face is lit from below, still blank. A single gold tear sits under one dot eye. The small text sits at the bottom right in typewriter.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: ENCORE

Picture: Composed like a live album cover. The man sits inside the empty recycling bin at the curb, only his head, shoulders and arms above the rim. He wears old foam headphones plugged into a black Walkman. He headbangs, so his long grey hair whips up in a wide black fan above his bald head. Both hands throw devil horns. His face is blank. Behind him, the empty street and a row of quiet black houses. Gold stage spotlights beam down on him from the sky in a V shape, as if an arena watches. Nobody is there. ENCORE runs across the top in bold cut-paper capitals, black with a gold offset edge.
