Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline, bold cut-paper capitals)
Picture: The front porch of a red-brick semi-detached, straight on, low and flat, three steps and a railing. The man stands on the top step in black: rubbery simplified body, thick even outline, two dot eyes, no mouth, a cardboard box held flat against his hip. Beside him, the same height, stands the machine in pink: a smooth legless column shaped like a woman with no arms and no face, a plain glowing ring where a head would be, a power cord running out of her base, down the steps and off the bottom edge of the page. He looks at the door. The ring looks at nothing. The title sits across the top of the page in black capitals. Everything else is bare paper. The pink layer is a clean 2 mm low and left, so her outline slides off the porch and the ring doubles.

## Page 2
Words: He still had a key. (small typewriter, bottom left)
Picture: The front hall from inside, one flat wall, one door, one baseboard outlet. He is black, in the doorway, holding the door open with his back, the box still under his arm, dot eyes pointed down at the floor. The machine in pink glides past him into the hall, cord dragging behind her across the threshold, the plug already in his free hand and reaching for the outlet. Two unopened boxes on the floor in black outline. No furniture, no pattern, no lamp. Most of the page is empty paper above their heads. The pink cord misregisters far enough that it appears to pass through his leg.

## Page 3
Words: GREAT QUESTION. (bold cut-paper capitals, pink, upper right) / He had not asked one. (small typewriter, bottom left)
Picture: A kitchen counter, edge-on and flat, two cupboard doors behind it. He is black, side view, standing with a mug halfway to his closed mouth, dot eyes forward, completely still. The machine in pink sits on the counter beside him, her ring lit, her cord looping down the cupboard to the outlet. The two words come out of her ring as a plain pink rectangle with black capitals inside it, floating in the empty air above the counter. Nothing else in the frame. The pink rectangle sits 2 mm off its black letters, so the words hang half outside their own box.

## Page 4

Picture: A small square table seen straight on, two chairs, two plates. He sits in the left chair in black, leaning forward, one hand open in the air mid-sentence, mouth a small black oval, the only open mouth in the book so far. His plate has food on it, drawn as three flat black shapes. The right chair holds the machine in pink, ring dark, cord trailing under the table to the wall. Her plate is empty and drawn in pink only. The room behind them is nothing but paper and a single black baseboard line. Misregistration puts her chair slightly through the table leg.

## Page 5

## Page 6

A green bin at the curb in black outline with a raccoon sitting on the lid, black, dot eyes, facing the pink window. No people in the frame at all. Wide empty paper above the roofline.
