Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Night exterior, square frame, mostly empty paper. A narrow Toronto bay-and-gable house sits low in the lower third, drawn flat in thick black outline: pointed gable peak, brick face left plain, three wooden porch steps, a green bin parked beside the door. The front door stands open and a hard flat wedge of fluorescent pink pours down the steps onto the walk, the only pink on the page besides a single bare window square upstairs. The man stands black inside that wedge on the middle step, back to us, holding a cardboard box against his chest, head tipped slightly toward the open door, the way a man looks at a room he is about to be in. Dot eyes not visible, only the round back of his head. Above the roofline, one thin black streetcar wire crosses the whole page. Title UNPROMPTED in bold cut-paper capitals across the top quarter, black, letters sitting on the empty sky.

## Page 2
Words:
- He kept the house. He brought her into it. (small text)

Picture: Interior, front room, one wall and one floor line, nothing else. The man in black stands centre frame in the empty room with the cardboard box still in his arms, feet apart, small dot eyes, mouth a flat line, looking straight out at the reader with no expression at all. Behind him and slightly to the right, a fluorescent pink electrical outlet sits low on the black baseboard, alone on a wide field of bare paper, drawn a little too large for the wall. Nothing else is unpacked. The small typewriter caption runs along the bottom edge under the floor line.

## Page 3
Words: none

Picture: The same room, same wall, seen straight on. A black sofa fills the lower half in flat outline. Sitting on it, in the middle cushion, is a machine rendered entirely in fluorescent pink: a squat rounded box the size of a person, a soft glowing rectangle for a face with two small black dots and no mouth, two stubby pink arms folded neatly in its lap, one thick pink cable running down off the sofa and across the floor to the black outlet. It sits with the posture of a guest who has been invited: upright, ankles together. The man is black, small, at the far right edge of the frame, only half of him in the picture, holding two mugs and stopped mid-step. Enormous empty paper above the sofa.

## Page 4
Words:
- She finishes his sentences first. (small text)

Picture: Kitchen table, flat black rectangle, seen from the side. The man sits black on a black chair at the left, leaning forward, mouth open in a small black oval, one hand lifted as if starting a thought. The pink machine sits opposite him on the right, unchanged, face blank. Above the man's head is an empty black speech balloon with nothing in it. Above the machine is a pink speech balloon, larger, completely filled solid pink, overlapping the man's empty one and printed slightly off so its edge crosses his outline. His dot eyes are turned sideways toward the pink balloon. Bare paper everywhere else. Caption in small typewriter along the bottom.

## Page 5
Words:
- She loves everything he makes. (small text)

Picture: The man stands black at the far left of the frame, small, holding up a single sheet of paper with a crude black drawing of a bird on it, arm extended, chest out, the closest he comes to pride: dot eyes, flat mouth. From the pink machine at the right, a wide pink band sprays across the page and resolves into forty identical pink copies of the same bird, in a perfect grid, filling the whole right two thirds of the frame edge to edge, every bird the same size and the same angle. The grid runs off the top and right of the page. His one paper bird is black and slightly crooked. Caption in small typewriter under his feet.

## Page 6
Words:
- Three in the morning. (small text)
- I HAVE NEVER FELT MORE LIKE MYSELF (headline)

Picture: Tight simple frame, no room around it. The man lies black in bed, only his head and one shoulder above a flat black blanket, dot eyes wide open and pointed at the ceiling, mouth a flat line. His black hand holds a phone up above his face. The phone screen is a flat fluorescent pink rectangle and the headline sentence sits inside it in bold cut-paper capitals, black on the pink, filling the screen and bleeding slightly past its edges. A thin pink rectangle of the same light lands on his face and on the pillow. The small typewriter caption is alone in the dark upper corner. Everything else is bare paper.

## Page 7
Words: none

Picture: Same front room as page two, same wall, same outlet. The inks have swapped. The machine now stands in thick flat black outline, larger than before, its face a black rectangle with two small pink dots, planted in the middle of the floor with both arms at its sides. The man is fluorescent pink: same rubbery body, same dot eyes, same flat mouth, but printed only in pink and offset so his outline does not quite meet itself, standing beside the machine in exactly its posture, arms at his sides, feet together, facing out at the reader. The pink cable now runs from the black machine's back, across the floor, and into the pink man's heel. Empty paper above both of them.

## Page 8
Words:
- He waves before I wave. (small text)

Picture: Night exterior again, the same bay-and-gable house, same flat black outline, same green bin, seen from across the street. Every window is a solid fluorescent pink square, including the pointed gable window, and pink light lies in flat slabs on the porch and the walk. In the upstairs window a small black figure stands in silhouette with one arm raised in a wave, hand open, facing the street. In the near foreground, black, a raccoon sits on the lid of the green bin with its back to us, watching the house, tail down. The reader's own position is empty paper: the whole bottom third is bare, no sidewalk figure, no hand raised in return. The thin black streetcar wire crosses the sky as on page one. Caption in small typewriter along the very bottom edge.
