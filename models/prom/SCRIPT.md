Ink: metallic gold

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. The girl sits in the middle of the square, alone on off-white paper. She is the moon: her whole head is a flat gold full disc with three black crater dots as freckles, small black dot eyes, and a short flat black mouth. Her rubbery body wears a gold prom dress with a black outline. She sits on nothing, knees together, hands flat in her lap, a black phone face up on her knee. Five small black star shapes float around her. Blank expression, eyes on the phone. The gold layer sits about 2 mm off the black outlines.

## Page 2
Words:
- small text: Prom is Friday. Nobody has asked the moon.

Picture: The moon girl floats at the top right, full and gold, arms at her sides, legs dangling. Her dot eyes look down. At the bottom edge sits a small flat black town: six box houses, a church spire, and a school gym with a flat roof. Every house window is a small gold square. The rest of the page is empty paper. The small text sits bottom left under the town.

## Page 3
Words:
- headline: PROM?
- small text: She did the lighting.

Picture: A boy in a black suit kneels on a lawn at the bottom left and holds up a big white sign with PROM? in black cut-paper capitals. A girl in black leans out of a balcony at the right, hands on her cheeks. The moon girl hangs in the top corner, small and gold, and a flat gold cone of light falls from her face onto the couple like a stage spotlight. Her arms hang straight down. Dot eyes, flat mouth, no reaction. The small text sits under the lawn.

## Page 4
Words:
- small text: Howled at. Photographed. Written about. Not asked.

Picture: The moon girl, full and gold, floats at top centre with her hands folded in front of her dress. Below her, on one black hill, stand three figures in a row, all faces tilted up at her: a black wolf with its mouth open in a howl, a tourist holding up a phone with a tiny gold moon on its screen, and a man in a scarf writing in a notebook. The moon girl looks straight out at the reader, not at them. The small text sits along the bottom.

## Page 5
Words:
- small text: By Thursday she is a sliver.

Picture: The moon girl alone in the middle of empty paper. She is now a thin gold crescent: her head is a narrow gold sliver shape with one dot eye, and her body curls around her knees in the same curve, back to the reader's left. Her phone lies beside her, face down, black. One black star nearby. She is small on the page. The small text sits low and right.

## Page 6
Words:
- headline: A NIGHT UNDER THE MOON
- small text: Friday. They made a cardboard one.

Picture: Inside the school gym, seen from the back of the room. A banner across the top reads A NIGHT UNDER THE MOON in black cut-paper capitals. Under the banner, a big cardboard moon hangs on two strings: a flat gold circle with a smiley face drawn on and a visible black tape X on one side. Six black couples slow-dance under it. In the high gym window at the top right, the real moon girl presses her thin gold sliver face to the glass. Dot eye, flat mouth. The small text sits at the bottom.

## Page 7
Words:
- headline: PROM?
- small text: So she asks the Earth.

Picture: The Earth fills the bottom half of the page: a huge round boy drawn as a black-outlined circle with flat black continent shapes, two small dot eyes, rubbery arms resting on his belly. He looks up. The moon girl, still a gold sliver, floats just above his head and holds a white sign with PROM? in black cut-paper capitals, the same sign shape as page 3. Her arms are straight out, holding the sign right at his face. Both faces blank. The small text sits in the top left corner.

## Page 8
Words:
- small text: "I thought we were already going."

Picture: The moon girl, full and gold again, and the Earth boy, now wearing a small black bow tie, slow-dance in the middle of the page, her hands on his shoulders, his hands at her waist. A thin dotted gold ellipse loops around both of them as an orbit line. A tiny black gym sits on top of the Earth's head, with a very small cardboard moon on its roof. Both faces blank, dot eyes half closed. The quoted small text sits under the Earth as his line, with a short black tail pointing up to him. Generous empty paper around them.
