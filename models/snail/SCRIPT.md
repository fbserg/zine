Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED
Picture: One snail sits dead centre on bare paper, side view, facing right. Its body is a black-outlined rubbery blob with two eyestalks and small dot eyes, face blank. Its shell is a flat fluorescent pink spiral with a thick black outline. On top of the shell sits a pink square pillow, balanced. The snail looks straight out at the reader. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold black cut-paper capitals. Nothing else is on the page. The snail looks the same on every page after this one.

## Page 2
Words:
- headline: GUESTS TODAY.
- small text: Not tomorrow. Today.
Picture: A plain white wall with a black-outlined wall calendar hung in the upper left. One square on the calendar has a fat pink circle scribbled around it. The snail sits on the floor below, bottom right, head tilted up at the calendar. Its eyestalks are stretched very tall, both eyes fixed on the circle. The face is blank. The headline GUESTS TODAY. sits across the top in black. The small text sits under the snail in black typewriter.

## Page 3
Words:
- small text: Fluff the pillows.
Picture: A black-outlined couch fills the lower half of the frame. It holds three pink pillows. The snail lies flat on the middle pillow, pressed into it face down, like a person on a trampoline mid-bounce. The pillow puffs up pink around its edges. A round black wall clock hangs top left with no numbers, hands at nine o'clock. A window top right shows a small pink sun low in the corner. The small text sits at the bottom left in black typewriter.

## Page 4
Words:
- small text: Water the plants.
Picture: Three black-outlined flower pots stand in a row on the floor, each with one tall black leafy plant. The snail sits beside the second pot. A pink watering can balances on top of its shell, tipped forward. Pink water drips down, mostly onto the snail's own head, not the plant. The snail's face stays blank. The same round clock hangs top left, hands now at three o'clock. The window top right shows the pink sun high and in the middle. The small text sits bottom left in black typewriter.

## Page 5
Words:
- headline: VACUUM.
Picture: Bird's-eye view of an empty room floor, mostly bare paper. A black upright vacuum cleaner stands in the lower right, the snail pushing it forward with its head. Behind the snail, a long glossy pink slime trail loops around the whole room in big circles and ends under the snail's body. The vacuum has cleaned nothing. Every spot the vacuum passed is covered by fresh pink trail. The window in the top corner is now solid black with a small pink moon. The headline VACUUM. sits top left in bold black cut-paper capitals.

## Page 6
Words:
- headline: DING DONG
- small text: Coming!
Picture: A long empty hallway runs from the bottom of the page to a small black front door at the very top. The hallway walls are two thin black lines. The snail sits at the bottom, at the start of the hallway, facing the door, a thin pink trail just starting behind it. The door looks very far away. A small window beside the door is black with a pink moon. DING DONG sits in bold black capitals next to the door. The small text Coming! sits beside the snail in black typewriter.

## Page 7
Words:
- small text: We waited. Bye.
Picture: The front door stands wide open, filling the left half of the frame. Outside, the sky is bare paper with a pink sun rising at the horizon line. Nobody is there. On the doormat lies a small folded note. The snail sits on the doormat, body touching the note, eyestalks drooping forward to read it. Its face is blank. A very long pink trail runs back into the house behind it and off the page. The small text is the note, printed in black typewriter on the note paper, large enough to read.

## Page 8
Words:
- headline: A SNAIL IS COMING FOR YOU.
Picture: A flat black two-lane highway runs straight from the bottom of the page to a vanishing point near the top, with a pink dashed centre line. The snail sits small on the pink centre line in the lower third, seen from behind, heading up the road toward the horizon. Its pink shell faces the reader. The rest of the page is bare paper. The headline sits across the top in bold black cut-paper capitals.
