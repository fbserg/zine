Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Cover. A single enormous sheet of paper fills almost the whole square, standing upright, taller and wider than the figure behind it. The sheet is a flat fluorescent pink rectangle, edges slightly soft, sitting 2 mm off from the thin black outline drawn around it. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the middle of the pink in bold black cut-paper capitals, nothing else on the sheet. Of the character we see only two rubbery black hands gripping the top corners from behind and two stubby legs and shoes below the bottom edge, heels together, toes turned slightly out. No face. Bare off-white paper above and to the sides.

## Page 2
Words: i love a big piece of paper (small text)

Picture: Full figure in profile, walking left to right along a sidewalk, seen from the side. The character is a short rubbery body with a round head, two black dot eyes, no mouth, thick even black outline. It carries the giant pink sheet flat against its front, arms out wide, so the sheet hides its torso and the head pokes over the top. The sheet is pink, everything else black line on bare paper. A green garbage bin at the far right edge of the frame, drawn in black outline only, cut off by the page edge. The small typewriter line sits low in the empty paper under the figure's feet. Expression blank, eyes forward.

## Page 3
Words: (none)

Picture: Same street, wider shot, figure now small and centred. The pink sheet has caught the wind and bowed into a taut curve, still gripped at both corners, lifting the character onto its toes so the shoes are just clear of the ground. The body is stretched slightly, arms straight, head tipped back. Pink: the curved sheet only. Black: figure, shoes, one leaning hydro pole at the left edge, a flat horizon line. Nothing else in the frame. Most of the page is empty paper above the figure.

## Page 4
Words: this is mostly on purpose (small text)

Picture: The character is airborne, about a metre up, feet trailing behind, still holding both corners of the pink sheet which is now fully belled out above and behind it like a sail. Drawn from the side, dead centre, tilted maybe fifteen degrees. Face unchanged: two dots, blank. A red streetcar passes along the bottom edge of the frame, only its top half visible, printed as a flat pink shape with black windows and black pole. The line of small type sits in the empty paper at the very bottom, under the streetcar roof.

## Page 5
Words: (none)

Picture: Very wide, very empty. The pink sheet is gone, a small pink lozenge high in the top right corner, tumbling, far away. The character runs across the lower third of the frame, small, arms up and forward, both shoes off the ground, head tilted up and back to watch the sheet. Black line only for the figure and a flat horizon. One black bird shape, simple, in the upper left. The whole middle of the page is bare off-white paper.

## Page 6
Words: i was finished with it anyway (small text)

Picture: The character stands waist-deep in flat water, facing the reader, dead centre. The water is a single flat pink band across the lower half of the page, its top edge straight, offset 2 mm from the black line that marks the shore behind. The figure's arms hang. In its right hand, held up and out of the water, is one small torn pink corner of the sheet, dripping. Hair or head shape flattened wet. Eyes still two dots, blank, level with the reader. A Canada goose stands in the water to the left, black outline, not looking. Far behind on the horizon, a single thin black vertical spike among three small rectangles. Small type across the empty paper above the figure's head.

## Page 7
Words: (none)

Picture: Interior, or as close as this book gets to one: the character sits on the floor at the bottom left of an otherwise empty square, knees up, one shoe on and one shoe missing, a pink puddle spreading under it. In front of it, propped against the right edge of the frame, is a fresh sheet of pink paper, blank, clearly larger than the first one, its top corner running off the top of the page. The character looks at it. Black: figure, the outline of the new sheet, nothing else. No furniture, no room, no walls. Enormous empty paper between the two of them.

## Page 8
Words: i have learned so much (small text)

Picture: Back on the sidewalk, same profile as page 2, walking left to right, but the new pink sheet is so large it exceeds the frame on three sides, top, right and bottom, so we see only the black grip of two hands at its lower left corner and one bare foot and one shoe stepping out beneath it. Wet footprints behind, small flat pink marks in a short trail. The pink sheet fills roughly three quarters of the square. The head is not visible. The small typewriter line sits in the narrow strip of bare paper at the far left, vertical space around it, alone.
