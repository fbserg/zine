Ink: metallic gold

Character, same on every page: a small gold hoop earring, printed solid in the gold ink, with two small black dot eyes on the hoop and thin rubbery black arms and legs. It never changes expression. Word total for the book: 23.

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. Square page, mostly off-white paper. The top third is one flat black slab: the underside of a bed, with four stubby black bed legs coming down. Below it, a floor line in thin black. In the lower middle, small and alone, the gold hoop earring sits on the floor with its legs out straight, arms at its sides, dot eyes staring at the reader, blank. The headline UNPROMPTED runs in bold cut-paper black capitals across the bottom edge. The gold sits about 2 mm off its black outline.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: It fell on a Tuesday.

Picture: A tall white page. At the top left corner, a black ear in simple profile and a strand of black hair cut off by the page edge. Halfway down the page, the gold earring falls straight down, arms up over its head, legs together, face blank as if waiting for a lift. Three short black speed lines above it. At the bottom, the edge of a black mattress and the dark gap under it. The small typewriter text sits in the empty right margin.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: Under the bed was fine.

Picture: The whole page is black, the dark under the bed. A flat off-white strip along the bottom is the floor. On it, the gold earring sits cross-legged. Next to it, in black outline filled with paper: one dust bunny, one lost coin, one tiny sock. The earring holds the coin like a plate on its lap. Its dot eyes are the same as always. The small text sits in paper-white type reversed out of the black at the top.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: She looked once.

Picture: Same view as page 3, black under the bed, floor strip at the bottom. A black hand and forearm reach in from the right edge, holding a phone torch. The torch beam is a flat gold wedge that sweeps across the floor. The wedge stops one finger short of the earring, which sits at the left edge in the dark, arm raised in a small wave. Face blank. The small text sits in the top right corner.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text: She bought new ones.

Picture: View from under the bed looking out at the room. The top edge of the frame is the black bed underside. Out in the bright room, two black shins and feet stand facing away. A black shopping bag hangs beside one foot, and a small open box on the floor shows two new gold hoops, plain, with no faces. Under the bed in the lower left corner, the earring lies on its stomach, chin on its hands, watching the box. The small text sits in the middle of the empty floor.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: Years.

Picture: Under the bed again, black all around, floor strip at the bottom. It is a stiff family portrait. The dust bunny is now as big as a dog and sits in the middle. The tiny sock hangs on the black wall like a framed picture. The gold earring stands upright at the dust bunny's side with one arm around it, dot eyes straight at the reader. A tiny gold paper calendar on the floor has many black strike marks. The single word sits low in the right corner.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text: She moved.

Picture: A bare room seen from above, mostly empty paper. The bed is gone. Its outline stays as a crisp clean rectangle in the floor, with a thin black frame of dust around it. In the exact centre of the rectangle, the gold earring stands small and alone, arms at its sides, looking up at the reader. Beside it sits the dust bunny. An open black door is at the top edge of the page. The small text sits in the bottom left corner.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: NOBODY ASKED

Picture: A new bedroom in side cross-section. The top half shows a new bed in black outline, with the woman asleep on it as a simple rubbery black shape under a flat gold blanket, eyes closed, blank. Two new gold hoops with no faces hang from her ear. The bottom half is the black space under the new bed. There sits the gold earring, next to a tiny black suitcase and the dust bunny. The earring's dot eyes face the reader, exactly as on the cover. The headline sits in bold cut-paper capitals across the bottom paper strip.
