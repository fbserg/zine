Ink: Fluorescent Orange

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline, cover title)
Picture: A man stands centered on plain off-white paper, three-quarter view, black outline body in a plain coat, dot eyes looking straight out. His moustache is drawn entirely in the second ink, a flat orange strip pressed above his lip, with a thin coiled elastic looping back to one ear, also orange. Everything else on the page is black line only: no shading, no background. His posture is upright and mildly pleased with himself, shoulders back, hands in pockets. The title sits above him in bold cut-paper capitals.

## Page 2
Words: none.
Picture: A cramped bathroom mirror scene, sink and tiled wall kept as flat black outline rectangles with no texture. The man leans close to his own reflection, two fingers pressing the orange moustache and its coiled elastic onto his lip. His reflected face mirrors the gesture exactly. A toothbrush sits in a cup on the sink ledge, drawn black. His expression is blank and businesslike, dot eyes fixed on his own reflection, the small daily ritual of putting on a disguise treated like brushing teeth.

## Page 3

In profile at the frame's edge, his moustache is visible, slightly crooked from the damp, elastic showing at his ear. His stride is unhurried, hands back in his pockets.

## Page 5
Words: Here. (small text, spoken)
Picture: Inside a Toronto streetcar, stanchion poles and a hanging pull cord drawn in flat black, ordinary bench seats left plain. The man stands, one hand open and extended toward his now-empty seat, offering it to an elderly rider approaching with a cane, shown only from behind. His moustache has begun peeling at one corner, a thin sliver of orange lifting away from his lip, unnoticed by him. His small word sits near his head in a plain typewriter caption. His dot-eyed expression stays flat and unbothered, as if this is routine.

## Page 6

## Page 7
Words: none.

The moustache dangles from one corner of his lip, mostly detached, the orange strip hanging loose. A cat has joined him on the step, sitting close, its head tilted up, staring with open interest directly at the dangling moustache. The street beyond is empty and quiet, nothing else drawn. His dot eyes stay forward, blank, unaware of the cat's attention.

## Page 8
Words: Oh. (small text, spoken)
Picture: The same bathroom mirror as page two, tile and sink unchanged, bookending the story. The man stands mid-peel, the orange fake moustache held loose in one hand, half removed from his lip. His posture and dot-eyed expression stay as flat and blank as every other page, the small word floating near his head in plain typewriter text, the only visible reaction.
