## Page 1
Words: headline: UNPROMPTED
Picture: Cover. A small round person with dot eyes and no mouth sits on an upturned milk crate in the lower left, drawn in thick black outline, knees together, a stubby pencil in one hand and a sheet of paper on their lap. Behind and above them, filling the upper two thirds of the page, a single huge flat fluorescent pink rectangle with one narrow slot in its front, like a vending machine seen too close, its edges shifted about 2 mm off from the thin black outline that should contain it. Nothing else. The headline UNPROMPTED in cut-paper capitals runs across the top of the pink shape in black. Generous paper margin below the figure.
