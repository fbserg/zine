Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square cover, mostly bare off-white paper. Centre left, a small rubbery figure in a cardigan and thick black outline stands in three-quarter view, holding one loose sheet of paper against her chest. Dot eyes, flat mouth, no expression. On the sheet, drawn in black, a plain pigeon. Behind her and slightly larger, a flat fluorescent pink slab the shape of a fridge with one horizontal slot near the top, standing on two short legs, leaning a few degrees toward her. The pink slab is misregistered 2 mm up and right, so a thin pink edge floats off its own shape. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold cut-paper black capitals, letters touching the top edge. Nothing else in the frame.

## Page 2
Words: pigeon, twenty minutes (small text)

Picture: A wide empty page. Bottom third only: the character sits cross-legged on the floor at a low table, seen from the side, drawing. Everything about her and her table is black outline on bare paper. Her hand rests flat on a single sheet; on the sheet, a black pigeon, one leg lifted. Her face is blank, tilted down. Beside the table, a mug and a stub of pencil. The small typewriter line sits under the sheet at the bottom edge, in black, like a note she made. No pink anywhere on this page. Two thirds of the page is empty paper above her head.

## Page 3
Words: here is your pigeon (small text)

Picture: Same low table, closer in, centred. The pink slab machine has pushed itself half into frame from the right, its slot open, and a fluorescent pink sheet slides out and lands across the corner of her black drawing. On the pink sheet, a pigeon, but with three legs and both eyes on the same side of the head. The character has not moved; she looks down at the pink sheet with the same blank dot eyes, one hand still flat on her own paper. Her figure, table and pigeon stay black outline. The machine, its sheet and its pigeon are all pink, misregistered 2 mm. The small typewriter line is printed in pink, floating just above the machine's slot.

## Page 4
Words: nine hundred pigeons (small text)

Picture: Full bleed flood. Fluorescent pink pigeons cover the entire square page edge to edge, flat shapes, all slightly different and all slightly wrong: extra legs, two beaks, a wing on backwards, one with no head. They overlap in a dumb even carpet with no gaps. Near the lower centre, one small black pigeon on a white sheet is still visible, half buried, the only thing on the page that is not pink. The character's black hand reaches in from the bottom edge, fingers spread over her sheet. The small typewriter line is pink, set in the top left corner over the pigeons.

## Page 5
Words: PIGEON (small text, repeated on the flyers)

Picture: Outdoors, daylight, a bare page with one vertical black telephone pole running its full height, slightly left of centre, thick with black staples. Overhead, two thin black streetcar wires cross the top of the frame. The pole is papered in fluorescent pink flyers layered four deep, each with the same clumsy pink pigeon and the word PIGEON in small type, each with a fringe of pull tabs at the bottom. One black-outlined sheet, her sheet with her black pigeon, is pinned under the pink layers; only its top corner and one pigeon eye still show. No character in the frame. Empty paper on both sides of the pole.

## Page 6
Words: the raccoon kept mine (small text)

Picture: Night, but no shading, just bare paper. The character sits on a curb at the bottom of the frame, knees up, hands empty, staring straight out at the reader with flat dot eyes. Everything about her is black outline. To her right, a black green-bin with its lid open and a raccoon standing on the rim, also black outline, holding her black pigeon drawing in both front paws and looking at it. A single fluorescent pink flyer lies flat in the gutter near her shoe, face up. The small typewriter line sits in black beside her, level with her shoulder. Wide empty paper above.

## Page 7
Words: what is that (small text)

Picture: Same curb, same night, now facing the other way. The character kneels on the sidewalk in profile with a fresh sheet flat on the concrete, pencil down, already drawing. The pink slab machine has come outside and stands at the left edge of the frame under a bare black streetlight pole, leaning in over her shoulder to look, slot half open, legs together and awkward. The machine is fluorescent pink, misregistered 2 mm. On her sheet, in black, the first lines of the machine: the slab, the slot, the two short legs. Her face is blank and close to the paper. The small typewriter line is pink, printed above the machine's slot.

## Page 8
Words: it sat very still (small text)

Picture: Same curb, wide, mostly empty paper. Right of centre the character stands and holds up her finished sheet toward the reader with both hands. On the sheet, in clean black outline, a portrait of the machine, slab and slot and two short legs, dead centre and calm. Left of centre and smaller, the actual machine sits on the curb in fluorescent pink, legs straight out in front of it, slot closed, holding its pose, misregistered 2 mm so a pink ghost edge sits beside it. No pink pigeons anywhere on this page. The character's face is blank. The small typewriter line sits in black along the bottom edge.
