Ink: metallic gold

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: The cover. The moon girl sits dead centre on a thin black rooftop line that runs across the lower third of the page. Her head is a big flat gold full moon with a thick black outline, two small black dot eyes and a short flat black mouth. Her body is rubbery and small, in a gold prom dress with a black outline. She sits with her knees together and her hands folded in her lap, facing us. Beside her on the roof is a small black corsage box, lid closed. Five tiny black stars dot the empty paper above her. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. The gold layer sits 2 mm off the black, so a thin gold sliver escapes her outline on one side.

## Page 2
Words:
- small text: Every night, she got ready.

Picture: The moon girl stands on the same black rooftop line, left of centre, in front of a small black-framed mirror propped against a chimney. Her gold moon head and gold dress fill the frame's attention. She holds a black lipstick up to her flat mouth, elbow high, face blank. Her gold high heels sit neatly beside her. Three tiny black stars in the paper sky. The small typewriter text sits in the bottom right corner of the paper.

## Page 3
Words:
- headline: PROM
- small text: TOTAL ECLIPSE

Picture: A single paper poster, tacked to the night sky with a black pin through a tiny black star, fills the middle of the page. On the poster, the headline PROM sits in black cut-paper capitals. Under it, the small typewriter text TOTAL ECLIPSE. Between the two words, a gold circle overlaps a solid black circle by half, like two faces pressed together to slow dance. In the bottom left corner, the moon girl's gold head peeks up from below the page edge, only the top half visible, dot eyes fixed on the poster.

## Page 4
Words:
- small text: Someone came to ask.

Picture: The moon girl sits on the rooftop line at the right side of the page, knees together, hands clasped at her chest, gold head tilted a little toward the left. From the left edge, a plump cloud floats in. The cloud is a paper-white shape with a thick black outline, two dot eyes and no mouth. It hides one short black arm behind its back, and a single gold rose pokes out from behind it. Everything else is empty paper. The small typewriter text sits along the bottom edge, centred.

## Page 5
Words:
- small text: It was a cloud.

Picture: Same rooftop, same framing as page 4. The cloud now hovers right in front of the moon girl and covers her whole head and most of her body. Only her gold high heels, her ankles, and two thin slices of gold moon at the cloud's edges show. The cloud faces us with dot eyes, holding the gold rose out toward the reader with one black arm. The girl's hands stick out from both sides of the cloud, still clasped, now pointing nowhere. The small typewriter text sits bottom centre.

## Page 6
Words:
- small text: Then more clouds.

Picture: A wide view. The moon girl sits small on the rooftop line at the far right, gold head, blank face, chin in one hand. From the left edge to her feet stretches an orderly queue of six black-outlined paper clouds, each a little different in size. Each cloud holds one gold rose. The front cloud stands on tiptoe. Short black rain dashes fall from the last cloud in line onto the roof. The girl looks straight out at us, not at the queue. The small typewriter text sits top left in the empty paper.

## Page 7
Words:
- small text: She practiced anyway.

Picture: The rooftop is empty of clouds. A pile of gold roses sits discarded by the chimney at the left. In the centre, the moon girl slow dances alone, one arm raised around the shoulders of nobody, the other arm on the waist of nobody, eyes closed as two short black dashes. On the right half of her gold moon face, a flat black semicircle covers the gold, the shadow of the partner who is not there. Her gold dress swings in one flat curve. The small typewriter text sits bottom left.

## Page 8
Words:
- small text: Next total eclipse:
- headline: 2044

Picture: The moon girl sits on the rooftop line in the lower left, exactly as on the cover: knees together, hands folded, gold moon head, dot eyes, flat mouth. Beside her, one small cloud now sits too, also facing forward, holding its gold rose in its lap. Neither looks at the other. On the far right horizon, very small, a gold sun with a black outline sinks below the rooftop line, facing away. Most of the page is empty paper. The small typewriter text sits top right, and the headline 2044 in large black cut-paper capitals sits directly under it.
