UNPROMPTED, an eight-page mini zine, riso: black plus one ink from fluorescent pink, fluorescent orange, blue, green, metallic gold. Flat shapes, deadpan.
One recurring character across all eight pages. The joke is in the drawing. Sixty words for the whole book, cover included.
A stranger gets it on one pass, cold. Most pages want one figure and paper.
Skip what a zine usually has: edition number, price, colophon.
A girl waiting to be asked to prom. She is the moon. Prom is the eclipse. So far only clouds come.
Everything else is yours.
