Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square page, mostly bare paper. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top third in bold cut-paper capitals, black, wide letter spacing, letters sitting on one line. Below it, centred and small, stands the whole character: a tall rubbery figure in a heavy open coat, thick even black outline, two dot eyes, a short flat mouth, arms straight down, feet together, facing the reader, expression blank. Black carries the figure, the coat, the title. The pink is one thing only: a flat rounded blob filling the torso inside the open coat, as if the coat were a window onto it. The pink layer sits about 2 mm down and to the left of the black, so a crescent of pink spills past the coat's edge onto the bare paper. No floor, no horizon, no other marks.

## Page 2
Words: Tuesday. (small text)

Picture: A plain interior with no walls drawn. Black: a rectangular table seen from a low three-quarter angle, one simple chair, the figure seated behind the table with hands in the lap and elbows tucked in, shoulders level, dot eyes aimed straight ahead, mouth a flat line. On the table, one blank square sheet squared up to the table edge, and one pencil laid parallel to the sheet. Pink: the torso blob only, still full, still nudged 2 mm off the black so it laps over the coat's edge. The word Tuesday. sits in small typewriter type in the bottom left corner, black. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 3
Picture: Close crop, the figure from the chest up, filling the middle of the page with paper above and below. Black: the head and shoulders, the coat collar, dot eyes looking straight out at the reader, mouth a short flat line pressed shut, both hands laid flat on the table edge at the very bottom of the frame. The pink has moved: the blob now fills the neck and the lower half of the head, pushing the cheeks out into two soft bulges that the black outline bows around. Misregistered 2 mm as always, so pink shows at the jaw. Nothing else in the frame, no table objects, no background.

## Page 4
Picture: The figure bent forward over the sheet, head low and turned down toward the paper, mouth open. Black: the figure in profile, the near edge of the table, the square outline of the sheet, the pencil pushed aside and off to one side. Pink: a single continuous stream leaving the open mouth and falling to the sheet, where it spreads into a growing pool. The pink layer's 2 mm offset makes the pool's edge cross the black outline of the sheet at the lower left. The pose is quiet, not violent: knees under the table, one hand steadying the paper, eyes shut as two short black dashes. No other objects on the page.

## Page 5
Words: ok. (small text)

Picture: The widest, emptiest page in the book. Black: the figure sat back upright in the chair, arms hanging straight down at the sides, shoulders dropped, dot eyes open, mouth a flat line, and the torso inside the open coat now completely bare paper with no pink in it at all. The table runs across the lower third. On it, the square sheet holds one big flat pink shape, an irregular blob, the largest pink area anywhere in the zine, offset 2 mm so it hangs past the sheet's black outline on two sides. The word ok. sits in small typewriter type near the bottom edge, black, well away from the figure. No walls, no floor, no clutter.

## Page 6
Words: 504 KING (small text), OPEN (small text)

Picture: A night street, drawn thin and low. Black: the figure walking left to right across the lower third, small, hands in coat pockets, torso empty, face blank; behind it a streetcar as a flat black side silhouette with its windows cut out as rectangles; one pole and two overhead wires as thin black lines; a raccoon sitting upright on a closed bin at the right edge, flat black, small dot eye. Pink: only the lit things, as flat rectangles: the streetcar's windows, the streetcar's roll sign, and one shop window at the left. Each pink rectangle sits 2 mm off its black frame so the light slides. The roll sign reads 504 KING and the shop window reads OPEN, both in small typewriter capitals, black, printed over the pink. The colour is out in the city now and not in him. Sky and road stay bare paper.

## Page 7
Picture: A bare wall, no furniture, no skirting, no window. Black: the figure seen from behind and a little small, both arms raised, pressing the square sheet flat to the wall with one short strip of tape at the top edge; the coat hangs; one heel is lifted. The sheet is drawn as a plain square outline. Pink: the blob on the sheet, unchanged from page 5, offset 2 mm so it laps over the sheet's outline and onto the wall. Nothing else is on the wall. Most of the page is empty paper.

## Page 8
Words: Wednesday. (small text)

Picture: The exact composition of page 2, same angle, same table, same chair, same blank square sheet squared to the table edge, same pencil laid parallel, figure seated with hands in the lap and a blank face. One change in black: the taped sheet from page 7 now hangs on the wall behind, small, upper right, drawn as a square outline. Pink appears twice: the blob on the hung sheet, and a single small dot in the figure's chest inside the open coat, no bigger than an eye, far smaller than the blob on page 2. Both pink shapes carry the 2 mm offset. The word Wednesday. sits in small typewriter type in the bottom left corner, black, in the same spot Tuesday. held. Everything else is empty paper.
