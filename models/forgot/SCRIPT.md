Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: A square of mostly empty paper. Low in the frame, a rubbery man in a bathrobe lies back in an armchair, legs out, arms slack over the arms of the chair, small dot eyes open, mouth a short flat line. He is drawn in thick even black outline. One index finger points up, and balanced on the fingertip is a small box the size of a sparrow, drawn in flat fluorescent pink with a black outline. A thin pink cord runs from the box off the top edge of the page. The title UNPROMPTED sits across the top third in bold cut-paper capitals, black. The box is the only pink object on the page and its pink layer sits 2 mm off its black outline.

## Page 2
Words: 8:40. He is awake. That is his part. (small text)

Picture: A bed seen flat from the side, black outline, empty paper all around it. The man lies under a black blanket, head on the pillow, dot eyes open, expression blank, arms down at his sides, not moving. Hovering over the bed in a neat row are four little pink arms on thin pink stalks coming in from off the right edge: one holds a loaded toothbrush, one a steaming mug, one a necktie already knotted, one an open newspaper. Everything the machine holds is flat fluorescent pink. The man, the bed, the pillow and the room are black only. The small typewriter line runs along the bottom edge in black.

## Page 3
Words: NEWS (headline)

Picture: The man sits upright in the armchair, seen straight on, holding a broadsheet newspaper open with both hands. The newspaper is a plain flat fluorescent pink rectangle, no columns, no headline, no text at all, a blank pink shape with a thin black outline around it. His dot eyes are aimed at it and his mouth is a flat line, perfectly content. On the chair arm beside him sits the small pink box, and one crisp black speech balloon comes out of the box with the word NEWS inside in bold cut-paper capitals. Paper is empty above and below.

## Page 4
Words: Stop. Walk. Bakery. Wave at her. (small text)

Picture: A bare street drawn as one long black ground line across the lower third. The man strolls left to right in his bathrobe and slippers, arms swinging, dot eyes forward, mouth flat, thoroughly happy. Three sign shapes stand along the street in flat fluorescent pink with black outlines: an octagon on a post, a square shop board, a long banner. All three are completely blank, no letters anywhere. The small pink box rides on his shoulder and a black speech balloon from the box carries the small typewriter line. His near hand is raised in a friendly wave, and the thing he is waving at is a fire hydrant.

## Page 5
Words: HA HA HA (headline)

Picture: A dinner table seen flat from the side, black outline, three rubbery people behind it. The two others are thrown back mid-laugh, mouths wide open black ovals, eyes squeezed to short black dashes. The man sits between them dead still, hands flat on the table, dot eyes level, mouth one short flat line, entirely at ease. The small pink box stands on the table in front of him, and from the box rises a large flat pink speech balloon with HA HA HA in bold cut-paper capitals in black inside it. The balloon is the biggest shape on the page. No food on the table.

## Page 6
Words: none

Picture: A wall, drawn as a single black line across the page, with a black socket on it. The pink cord lies on the floor with its plug out of the socket, curved and loose. The small box sits on the floor beside the plug with its pink fill gone, drawn now in black outline only, empty inside, the one page where the box is not pink. The man stands over it in his bathrobe, arms at his sides, looking down, dot eyes small, mouth a flat line, no alarm at all. Everything else on the page is bare paper.

## Page 7
Words: EVERYTHING IS FINE (headline)

Picture: The man stands alone, centre, feet apart, holding the blank pink newspaper up high in one fist like a town crier. Around him, filling the page at their own angles, float six or seven more blank flat pink shapes with black outlines: a book, a letter, a door plaque, a road sign, a phone screen, a shop board, all of them empty of letters. His mouth is open at last, a wide black oval, and from it comes a black speech balloon with EVERYTHING IS FINE in bold cut-paper capitals. His dot eyes are bright and he is having the time of his life. The pink layer misregisters hard here, so every blank shape has a pink edge sliding off its black outline.

## Page 8
Words: Hello? (small text)

Picture: An empty room, almost all bare paper, with one black line for the floor and the black armchair sitting empty near the centre. The small box is on the seat of the chair, lit pink again, with a black speech balloon and the small typewriter word inside it. The front door stands open at the right, a black rectangle. Far away through the doorway, tiny, drawn at one tenth the size, the man walks off with his back turned, one arm raised in a wave, and the thing he is waving at is another fire hydrant. No other objects in the room.
