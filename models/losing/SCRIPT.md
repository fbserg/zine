Ink: fluorescent orange

Rule for the illustrator: on every page, anything new is orange and anything old is black. The man never sees the old things. They stay on him or right behind him.

The man: short, rubbery, round bald head, small dot eyes, flat line mouth, knee-length paper-white coat with a thick black outline, black trousers, black shoes. His face is the same blank face on every page.

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: The man stands dead centre on empty paper, full figure, feet together, arms at his sides. His right hand holds a small shopping bag in solid orange with a folded top. He looks straight out at the reader with dot eyes and a flat mouth. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. Nothing else is on the page.

## Page 2
Words:
- headline: KEYS
- small text: Lost them. Bought more.

Picture: The man stands in the middle of the page and holds up a new orange key ring with three orange keys at eye level. He looks at the keys, not at the reader, face blank. His old black key ring, with three black keys, hangs from his coat pocket in plain view, swinging a little. The headline KEYS sits top left in black. The small text sits under his feet in black typewriter.

## Page 3
Words:
- headline: HAT
- small text: Lost it. Bought another.

Picture: The man stands side-on and faces right. A new orange bowler hat sits on his head, and his orange keys hang from his left hand. His old black bowler hat hangs down his back from a black chin string around his neck. The old black keys still hang from his coat pocket. He looks ahead with a blank face and no idea about the hat on his back. The headline HAT is top right in black. The small text is bottom left.

## Page 4
Words:
- headline: GLASSES
- small text: Lost them. Bought more.

Picture: Close on the man from the chest up, facing the reader. New round orange glasses sit on his nose. His old round black glasses sit pushed up on his forehead, just under the brim of his orange hat. The black chin string of the old hat is visible at his neck. His dot eyes look out through the orange lenses, mouth flat. The headline GLASSES runs along the top in black. The small text sits bottom centre.

## Page 5
Words:
- headline: UMBRELLA
- small text: Lost it. Bought another.

Picture: The man stands full figure in rain, drawn as short black vertical dashes over the whole page. He holds a new open orange umbrella over his head. His old black umbrella, closed, hangs from the back of his coat collar by its hooked handle. He wears the orange hat and orange glasses, with the black glasses on his forehead and the black hat on his back. The black keys hang from his pocket. His face is blank and dry. The headline UMBRELLA sits across the top above the rain in black. The small text sits at the bottom.

## Page 6
Words:
- headline: DOG
- small text: Lost him. Bought another.

Picture: The man walks left to right across the middle of the page with the closed orange umbrella under his arm. He leads a small new dog in solid orange on an orange leash in front of him. Directly behind him, the old dog in solid black follows at his heels and holds its own black leash in its mouth. The old dog looks up at the man with dot eyes. The man looks ahead, face blank. He still carries every old black item: hat on his back, glasses on his forehead, umbrella on his collar, keys at his pocket. The headline DOG is top left in black. The small text is bottom right.

## Page 7
Words:
- headline: HOUSE
- small text: Lost it. Bought another.

Picture: A plain square house with a triangle roof, one door, and two windows, in solid orange, sits in the centre of the page. The man stands in front of its door, small, holds up an orange key, and faces the reader with a blank face. The orange dog sits at his feet. Directly behind the orange house stands the old house in solid black, the same shape but a little bigger, so its black roof and walls stick out above and on both sides of the orange house. The black dog sits in the black doorway gap that shows at the side. The headline HOUSE runs across the top in black. The small text is bottom centre.

## Page 8
Words:
- headline: ME
- small text: Lost myself. Bought another.

Picture: Two identical men stand on empty paper in the same pose, facing the reader. In front is a new man drawn entirely in orange: orange outline, orange hat, orange glasses, orange umbrella under his arm, orange keys in hand, orange dog at his feet. Directly behind him, a little up and to the left so he peeks out, stands the old man drawn entirely in black with every old black item on him: hat on his back, glasses on his forehead, umbrella on his collar, keys at his pocket, black dog at his feet. Both men have dot eyes and flat mouths. Both dogs stare out too. The headline ME sits top right in black. The small text sits bottom centre.
