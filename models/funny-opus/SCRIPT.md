Ink: fluorescent pink

Word count, whole book: 8 of 60.

Character: a rubbery bald man in a sweater, drawn in thick black outline with small dot eyes and no mouth. His pink is one flat shape: sweater and a round spot on each cheek. It sits about 2 mm off his black line, like the rest of the print. He is the only thing in the book that owns any pink, and his pink is a separate figure that can move on its own. All pink text is spoken by his pink. All black text is spoken by him.
