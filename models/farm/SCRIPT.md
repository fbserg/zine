Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square page, mostly bare paper. The title sits across the top third in bold cut-paper capitals, black. Along the bottom third a single thin black ground line runs edge to edge. Deb walks left to right on that line, full figure, small, rubbery limbs, thick even black outline, two dot eyes, mouth a short flat line, no expression. Her right hand carries a plain plastic bucket at knee height. She is mid-stride and unhurried. At the extreme right edge, level with her knee, one goat head and neck push into the frame: flat solid fluorescent pink with its own black outline, the pink sliding 2 mm down and left off the black. Deb does not look at it. Everything else on the page is paper.

## Page 2
Words: (none)

Picture: Night on the porch of a farmhouse, drawn as flat black shapes: the corner of the house on the left, a plain rail, four steps. Four friends sit in a row on the boards, rubbery bodies, dot eyes, blank faces, all four heads turned the same way toward the right edge at nothing. Deb stands at the top of the steps with one foot already on the first step down, bucket in her left hand, facing right, blank. Behind and above her a bare bulb hangs. The bulb glow and the rectangle of light it throws on the boards print as flat fluorescent pink, offset 2 mm from the black porch so the light lies crooked on the floor. The whole right half of the page is empty paper.

## Page 3
Words: back in a sec (small text)

Picture: A very wide empty field. One low black horizon line across the page, high up, and nothing else on the ground. Deb is small and centred, seen from behind, walking away from us into the paper, bucket hanging from her hand, arms straight, body a simple black silhouette shape with the outline weight kept even. Far off on the horizon, a flat fluorescent pink band glows above the line: the city. No stars, no texture, no grass. The four typewriter words sit small in the bottom left corner with no balloon and no tail.

## Page 4
Words: (none)

Picture: Close to the ground at a wire fence. Three black fence posts and two straight wires cross the page at chest height. Deb stands at the left post facing right along the fence, one hand resting on the post top, body relaxed, dot eyes, flat mouth, bucket still in her other hand. Behind her, filling the middle of the page in a tidy row, three goats face her back: flat solid fluorescent pink bodies with black outlines, the pink layer shifted 2 mm right so each goat leaks out of itself. The goats are not moving. Deb has not turned. Wide paper above them all.

## Page 5
Words: deb they're herding you (small text)

Picture: A nearly empty square. Deb runs from right to left across the lower middle, body tilted forward, both arms held straight down at her sides, legs wide apart, hair flat, face blank with the same two dots. The bucket is still in her hand and level. Behind her, in a clean wide arc that curves from the top right down to her heels, seven goats: flat fluorescent pink shapes with black outlines, all the same size, all upright, spaced evenly like printed marks rather than animals, the pink slipping 2 mm off every one of them. No ground line, no background, no motion streaks. The four typewriter words sit small in the top right corner, no balloon.

## Page 6
Words: (none)

Picture: The swamp. The lower half of the page is flat black water with a hard straight top edge and a few bare paper gaps cut into it for reeds. A tractor stands in the water, drawn as flat black shapes, big rear wheel, small front wheel, plain seat on a stalk. Deb sits on the seat with her knees pulled up and both arms around them, the bucket held in her lap, facing us, blank dot eyes, mouth a flat line. Around the tractor, standing on top of the black water rather than in it, eight goats in a ring: solid fluorescent pink, black outlined, offset 2 mm. Nobody reacts to this. The top half of the page is empty paper.

## Page 7
Words: how many are there / eleven (small text, two stacked lines)

Picture: Tight crop on Deb on the tractor seat, head and shoulders and knees only, drawn in black, filling the upper left of the page, blank as ever, the bucket rim visible at the bottom of her arms. The entire bottom edge of the page is a row of goat heads seen from behind and below, cropped by the frame, flat fluorescent pink with black outlines, packed shoulder to shoulder, more of them than the eye wants to count, the pink layer shifted 2 mm up so the heads sit above their own outlines. Between Deb and the heads, empty paper. The two typewriter lines sit small and stacked in the bottom left corner, no balloons.

## Page 8
Words: (none)

Picture: Dawn at the porch. The house corner, rail and steps return as the same flat black shapes, now with a flat fluorescent pink band of sunrise behind them across the full width of the page, offset 2 mm so it rides up over the roofline. The four friends lie asleep on the porch boards behind, rubbery black shapes, eyes drawn as short closed lines. Deb sits on the bottom step, facing us straight on, legs out, the bucket tipped over on its side in her lap with feed spilled across her knees. One goat, flat fluorescent pink with a black outline, stands beside her with its head down in the bucket, eating. Deb looks directly out at the reader, two dot eyes, mouth a flat line, exactly the same face as page 1.
