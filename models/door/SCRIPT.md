Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline, bold cut-paper capitals)

Picture: Square page, mostly bare paper. The title sits across the top in thick cut-paper capitals, black. Lower centre-left, one figure in profile facing right: rubbery simplified body, thick even black outline, two dot eyes, flat mouthless face, knitted hat, a tote bag hanging off the far shoulder, feet flat and parallel on an implied floor line. The near arm reaches straight out to the right, hand flat against the edge of an open door. The door is the only pink shape on the page: a plain flat rectangle swung toward the reader, printed 2 mm off the black outline that describes it, so a pink lip shows along one side. Beyond the doorway there is nothing, just paper. The figure's expression is blank. Nobody is coming.

## Page 2
Words: thanks, kid (small typewriter, near the man's head)

Picture: The same square frame, the figure drawn in exactly the same place and the same pose as the cover, same hat, same tote, same flat feet, hand still on the door. Through the doorway a man walks left to right mid-stride, black outline only: heavy coat, round nose, one paper grocery bag held against his chest, a loaf sticking out. He is looking ahead, not at the figure. The door stays pink, same rectangle, same 2 mm misregistration. The small typewriter line floats beside the man's head with no balloon. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 3
Words: thanks (small typewriter, near the man's head)

Picture: Identical frame, identical figure, identical pose, identical door, identical pink offset. The man passing through is the same man and much older: stooped forward, thinner, four strands of hair, the same round nose, a cane in one hand and the same paper grocery bag in the other, the loaf replaced by a single carton. He moves left to right in the same lane as before. Black outline only. The small typewriter word sits where the previous one sat. Bare paper above and below.

## Page 4
Words: thanks, mister (small typewriter, near the child's head)

Picture: Identical frame, identical figure, identical pose, hand on the same pink door with the same 2 mm slip. Through the doorway a small child rolls left to right on a kick scooter, black outline only, one foot up, the same round nose as the man, a backpack too big for them. They are past the figure already, not looking back. The typewriter line sits low, at the child's head height. The rest of the page is paper.

## Page 5
Words: none

Picture: Identical frame, identical figure, identical pose, identical pink door, identical misregistration. Nobody is in the doorway. Through it, blank paper. On the floor in front of the figure's feet lies one dry leaf, black, small. The figure's dot eyes look straight ahead at nothing. The page is the emptiest in the book: one figure, one door, one leaf, and a great deal of uncoated paper.

## Page 6
Words: none

Picture: Identical frame, identical figure, identical pose, arm still out, door still pink and still 2 mm off. Two changes only: a bird in black outline stands on the figure's outstretched wrist, and a small nest of black twigs sits in the crook of the same elbow, with a second bird settled in it. Pink has begun to leak past the door into the room as a flat pink drift of snow gathered along the floor line and over the figure's shoes. The figure's face is unchanged, blank, dot-eyed, looking at the empty doorway. No weather lines, no texture, flat shapes only.

## Page 7
Words: none

Picture: Identical figure, identical pose, identical height on the page, hand still on the door. The wall is gone. The door and its frame now stand free in open ground, a thin black rectangle of frame with the pink door panel hanging in it, still 2 mm off. The ground is a flat pink field that runs to the bottom edge, with three black grass strokes and one black sapling behind the figure. A deer in black outline walks left to right and goes through the open doorway anyway, though it could walk around. Two birds still on the arm. Sky is bare paper.

## Page 8
Words: thanks (small typewriter, near the child's head)

Picture: Identical figure, identical pose, same place on the page, same hat, same tote, hand held at exactly the same height. The black frame is gone and the black outline of the door is gone with it. Only the pink door panel remains, a flat pink rectangle floating in the air at the same 2 mm offset from a black outline that is no longer printed, so the colour sits alone on the paper. The figure's hand rests against nothing, an inch from the pink. Flat pink ground rises to the ankles. Through the space where the door used to be walks a small child in black outline, left to right, plain clothes, no nose in common with anyone, holding a jar. Blank expression on both of them.
