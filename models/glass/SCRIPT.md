Ink: blue

The character on every page: a plain straight-sided drinking glass, drawn as a thick black outline on bare paper. Two small black dot eyes sit in the upper part of the glass, above the water. Two thin rubbery black arms come out of its sides. No mouth, no legs. Its water is a flat blue shape with a dead-straight top edge. Unless a page says otherwise, the water fills exactly the bottom half. The blue layer sits about 2 mm off the black, so the water edge can overlap the outline a little.

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. The glass stands small in the lower centre of a big field of empty paper, on one thin black floor line. Its blue water fills exactly the bottom half. Its dot eyes look straight ahead at the reader, blank. Its arms hang at its sides. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold black cut-paper capitals. Nothing else is on the page.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: I am half empty.

Picture: Close on the glass, centred, larger than on the cover. Its blue water fills exactly the bottom half. Both dot eyes turn up toward the empty upper half above them, which is plain paper inside the black outline. The arms hang limp. The small typewriter text sits under the floor line. Everything else is empty paper.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: Nobody asked.

Picture: Wide shot. The glass is tiny at the far right end of a long black table line that crosses the whole page. The rest of the table is bare. Its blue water still fills exactly the bottom half. It faces left toward all that empty table, eyes blank. The small typewriter text sits low in the empty space on the left.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: I measured.

Picture: The glass stands centred and holds a black ruler upright against its own side with one arm. The ruler has plain black tick marks and no numbers. The blue water line meets the middle tick exactly, and the ruler is exactly as tall as the glass. The glass looks down at the ruler, eyes blank, very serious. The small typewriter text sits under the floor line.

## Page 5
Words: none.

Picture: Outdoors, nothing but the floor line. A flat black cloud hangs at the top of the page. Short blue raindrops fall in straight rows from the cloud. The glass stands in the rain with both arms raised out wide, eyes tilted up at the cloud. Its blue water is higher now, about three quarters. Lots of empty paper between the cloud and the glass.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: Too full.

Picture: The cloud is gone. The glass stands centred, arms stiff at its sides. Its blue water fills it to the brim and bulges up in a small dome above the rim. One blue drop runs down the outside of the glass. The dot eyes look sideways at that drop, blank. The small typewriter text sits under the floor line.

## Page 7
Words: none.

Picture: The glass leans hard to the right on its bottom edge, holding itself steady with one arm on the floor. A curved blue stream pours out over the rim and lands in a flat blue puddle on the floor line. The water inside is sinking back toward half. The dot eyes watch the water line inside the glass, careful and blank, like a person who pours medicine.

## Page 8
Words:
- Small text: Half empty.

Picture: Same framing, pose, and size as page 2. The glass stands upright and centred, blue water again exactly the bottom half. Both dot eyes turn up to the empty upper half, arms limp. The only change is a wide flat blue puddle on the floor right beside it, bigger than the glass. The glass does not look at the puddle. The small typewriter text sits under the floor line.
