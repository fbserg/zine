## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: A single goat, centred, square-on to the reader, standing on a thin black ground line low in the frame. Nothing else. The goat is drawn in thick even black outline, flat white body, one small black dot eye, rectangular pupil ignored in favour of the dot, ears out sideways, blank. Its eye-shine is a flat fluorescent orange oval sitting behind the head, so the orange layer slips off the eye by 2 mm and reads as a smear of light stuck to the skull. The title UNPROMPTED runs in bold cut-paper black capitals across the upper third, wide, letters nearly touching the left and right edges. Everything above the ground line is bare paper. The goat is not doing anything. It has been there a while.
