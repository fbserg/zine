Ink: fluorescent pink

The character, same on every page: a small pear-shaped person with a black bowl haircut, two dot eyes, a short flat line for a mouth, a big paper-white shirt with a thick black outline, black trousers, tiny black shoes. The character always holds the same pink stapled booklet. The face never changes.

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: The character stands in the lower middle of an empty square of paper, facing us, holding the pink booklet open at chest height with both hands. A plain black mic stand stands just to the left, the mic at mouth height. Under the character's feet is a flat pink oval of spotlight on the floor. Nothing else: no room, no audience. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. The pink booklet and the pink oval sit about 2 mm off their black outlines. Blank dot eyes look straight out at the reader.
