Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: A white goat, Maureen, stands side-on in the lower middle of an empty field, head turned to face the reader. Her body is a rubbery white shape with a thick black outline, small dot eyes, a flat line mouth, a short black beard, and two small black horns. Behind her and far to the right sits a small black barn. Its windows and open door are solid pink rectangles, and pink shows between its wall boards. The sky is plain paper. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals. Maureen looks calm and a little tired.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: Saturday. The barn had a party. Maureen stayed out.

Picture: Night. The barn fills the right half of the page, a black silhouette with every window blasting solid pink. Flat pink music notes and a pink party streamer spill out of the door. Rubbery black silhouettes of a cow, a pig, and a rooster dance in the windows with their arms up. Maureen stands alone on the far left, small, on plain paper, facing the barn with a blank expression. Her white body has no pink on it at all. The small text sits bottom left under her hooves.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: She took the keys.

Picture: Close on Maureen from the chest up, centred, on plain paper. A ring of tractor keys hangs from her mouth, the keys in black and the key fob in pink. Her dot eyes look straight at the reader. Her expression is completely flat. In the lower right corner, a black rooster in a pink cone party hat reaches up for the keys with one wing, too short and leaning at a bad angle. The small text sits at the top left.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: She held his feathers back.

Picture: Inside the barn, against plain paper with a single pink bulb hanging from a black cord at the top. The rooster, still in his pink party hat, leans head-first into a black bucket. Maureen stands behind him on her hind legs and holds his long black tail feathers up in one front hoof, like a ponytail. She looks off to the side, bored and polite. The small text sits bottom centre.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text: She brought water.

Picture: A pink pig lies face-down in a flat black pile of hay in the bottom half of the page, legs out, one pink streamer across its back. Maureen stands over the pig on the right. She holds a black tin cup of water in her mouth by the handle and lowers it next to the pig's ear. A single flat pink droplet hangs off the cup. Her face is patient and blank. Lots of empty paper above them. The small text sits top right.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: She ate the mess.

Picture: The morning-after floor of the barn, seen from the side. Crushed pink plastic cups, a pink paper banner, streamers, and a popped pink balloon lie in a line across the page. Maureen stands at the right end of the line with her head down, chewing. Half the line is already gone, and a long pink streamer runs from the floor straight into her mouth. Her belly is noticeably rounder than on earlier pages. In the background, the cow sleeps upright against a post with a pink lampshade on her head. The small text sits top left.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text: The refinery stayed up too.

Picture: Wide and quiet. A long flat black horizon line crosses the lower third. Far off on the horizon stands a row of thin black refinery stacks, each topped with a small solid pink flame. In the foreground on the left, Maureen stands on top of a black fence post, all four hooves together, round-bellied, facing the refinery. We see her from behind, beard and horns in silhouette. Pink streamer end still hangs from her mouth. The rest of the page is empty paper. The small text sits bottom right.

## Page 8
Words:
- Headline: NOBODY ASKED.

Picture: Morning. A solid pink circle of sun sits top right. On the left, the cow, the pig, and the rooster wake in a heap on the hay, squinting, one hoof or wing over each face, the rooster's pink party hat crushed flat. On the right, Maureen sleeps standing up, eyes shut as two short black lines, belly round as a ball, a tiny pink streamer tip still out of her mouth. A black tractor key ring sits on the ground between her hooves. The headline NOBODY ASKED. runs across the bottom in black cut-paper capitals.
