Ink: fluorescent pink

Black is every solid thing. Fluorescent pink is only light, water, and eyes. The 2 mm misregistration means nothing she sees sits quite on what is there.

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square cover, mostly empty off-white paper. Low centre, a small figure sits upright in the seat of a parked tractor, drawn flat and rubbery in thick black outline: bucket hat, jacket too big, both hands gripping the wheel at ten and two, two small dot eyes, no mouth. The tractor is black and blocky, seen straight from the side, one big rear wheel. Three goats stand on the ground around it, black bodies, facing the reader. Their eyes are pink dots. A pink wash sits under the tractor where the headlight should fall, offset down and left of the lamp. Title UNPROMPTED in bold cut-paper capitals across the top edge, black, letter-spaced wide, nothing else on the page.

## Page 2
Words: Someone has to stay straight. (small text)

Picture: Empty paper. Centre of the page, the figure stands alone in three-quarter view, feet together, holding one tractor key up at chest height between finger and thumb, looking at it. Dot eyes, blank face. Black outline only. A single pink highlight on the key, misregistered so the glow floats just off the metal. No background, no horizon, no other figures. Small typewriter text runs along the bottom edge under her.

## Page 3
Words: I'm driving. Seatbelts. (small text)

Picture: The tractor from the side, black, parked on a thin black ground line. The figure is in the seat, hands at ten and two, back straight, chin level, dot eyes forward. A goat stands on the footplate beside her, facing the same way, upright and calm, black with pink eyes. Both look dead ahead. Everything else is paper. Pink appears only as the headlight beam: one flat wedge thrown forward from the lamp, offset so it starts in front of the lamp and not at it. Small text sits beside the wheel.

## Page 4
Words: Oncoming. (small text)

Picture: View from behind her shoulders, over the wheel. The bottom third is the black silhouette of her hat, shoulders, and both hands on the wheel rim. The top two thirds is flat swamp: a wide pink field of standing water with black reeds cut into it as thin vertical strokes, and eight or nine pink ovals scattered across the water where the moon breaks up. The ovals read as headlights coming at her. Nothing moves. Her head is not turned. Small text at the top right corner.

## Page 5
Words: none

Picture: Side view again, same tractor, same paper, same ground line. A goat has walked into the space directly in front of the machine and stopped there, broadside, black, chewing, looking at the reader. The figure is half standing out of the seat with her full weight stamped down on the brake pedal, both arms locked on the wheel, hat tipped back, dot eyes wide open, mouth still absent. The tractor is exactly where it was on page 3, same distance from the left edge. Pink: the headlight wedge, unchanged, and the goat's eyes.

## Page 6
Words: Everybody stay calm. (small text)

Picture: Straight-on front view of the tractor, black, square, lamp centred. The figure is small in the seat behind the wheel, both palms raised flat toward the reader at shoulder height, hat crooked, dot eyes level, blank. Eleven goats fill the ground in front of the machine in a loose flat crowd, black bodies overlapping, all heads turned up at her, twenty-two pink eye dots. No grass, no fence, no barn. Pink otherwise only in the lamp glow, offset up and right so it lands on nothing. Small text under the front axle.

## Page 7
Words: Two hours to Barrie. (small text)

Picture: The widest page in the book and the emptiest. One thin black ground line low across the square. The tractor and the figure sit tiny at the far left, side on, her hands still at ten and two. The rest is bare off-white paper all the way to the right edge. One goat lies down in the far right corner, small and black, asleep. A single narrow pink wedge of headlight reaches out from the tractor and stops about a fifth of the way across, well short of everything. Small text sits alone in the middle of the empty space, not near either figure.

## Page 8
Words: We made it. (small text)

Picture: Morning. Same side view, same tractor, same spot on the same ground line, no pink anywhere except one flat band of low sun laid across the ground behind the wheels, misregistered so it runs under the machine rather than behind it. The figure is climbing down backward off the step, one foot reaching for the ground, hat pushed back, dot eyes, blank. A goat has taken the driver seat and sits there upright with its hooves near the wheel, black, pink eyes, facing forward. Nothing else on the page. Small text along the bottom edge.
