Ink: green

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square page, mostly bare off-white paper. A flat green band across the bottom fifth is the field. One figure stands dead centre in the lower half, small, drawn in thick even black outline: rubbery simplified body, sneakers, two dot eyes, mouth a short flat line, no expression. The figure holds a folded aluminium lawn chair upright beside it like a walking staff, frame in black outline, webbing straps solid green. Far right edge, standing on the green band, four goats in a straight row facing the figure, each a flat green body with black stick legs and one black dot eye, tiny against all that paper. The title UNPROMPTED runs across the top in bold cut-paper black capitals, letters filling the width. Green layer sits about 2 mm down and right of the black, so the goats and the chair webbing carry a soft ghost edge. No stars, no moon, nothing else.

## Page 2
Words: Ninety minutes north of Toronto. (small text, bottom left)

Picture: Wide empty field at night, the same flat green band low across the page, black paper elsewhere left as bare off-white. Five figures stand in a loose line in the middle distance, all in thick black outline, all identical rubbery bodies with dot eyes and flat mouths, none of them looking at each other. The second figure from the left holds the folded lawn chair, which marks our character for the rest of the book. Behind them at the right, an old tractor in flat black shapes, side on, big rear wheel, no driver, the wheel hubs and the seat pad in green. Nobody is doing anything. Small typewriter text sits low left with a lot of paper under it.

## Page 3
Words: Someone said the goats are loose. (small text, top right)

Picture: Same field, same camera. The four goats stand in the middle of the page in a straight row, evenly spaced, flat green bodies with black stick legs and black dot eyes, all facing the reader. Fix this exact position, size and spacing: the goats will be reprinted in the identical spot on pages 4, 5 and 7 and must not move a hair. Our character stands at the lower left corner, back to us, only the head, shoulders and the top of the folded chair in frame, black outline. The rest of the page is bare paper. The green layer's 2 mm offset gives every goat a faint second edge, which is the only thing about them that ever moves.

## Page 4
Words: THEY'RE COMING (headline, across the middle)

Picture: The four goats, same row, same size, same spot as page 3, chewing, flat green with black legs and dot eyes. Bold cut-paper black capitals cross the page at goat height, the letters overlapping the goats so the animals read through the word. Our character is a small black figure at the bottom edge, seen from behind, half out of frame, arms up, folded chair still in one hand. No motion lines anywhere. The joke is that nothing in the drawing has changed from the page before.

## Page 5
Words: I ran. The chair came with me. (small text, bottom right)

Picture: Same field. The four other friends stand exactly where page 2 left them, black outlines, flat and calm, dot eyes, hands at their sides. Our character runs across the foreground from right to left at full stretch, body stretched long and rubbery, legs wide, face still completely blank, the folded lawn chair clamped under one arm and sticking out past the frame edge. The goats sit in their fixed row in the upper right, same position and size as pages 3 and 4, green, unbothered. Green also fills the low field band. Everything else is paper.

## Page 6
Words: The swamp was ankle deep. It took an hour. (small text, two lines, bottom left)

Picture: A wide flat green shape covers the lower half of the page, the swamp, its edge a soft wobbling line with the 2 mm ghost. Three black reeds stick up. Our character stands in the middle of it, in profile, water at the ankles, one leg lifted high mid step, the unfolded lawn chair held over the head with both arms like a roof, chair frame black, webbing green. Blank dot eyes, flat mouth. No goats on this page at all. Upper half of the page is empty paper.

## Page 7
Words: HIGH GROUND (headline, top) / They were still coming. (small text, bottom right)

Picture: The tractor from page 2, side on, in flat black shapes with green wheel hubs and green seat pad, parked at the left. Our character stands balanced on the tractor seat, knees slightly bent, one hand on the steering wheel, the other holding the folded chair straight up in the air, head turned back over the shoulder, dot eyes and flat mouth, no fear in the face. Across the right of the page, the same four goats in the same row, same size, same spacing as pages 3, 4 and 5, green bodies, black legs, heads down in the grass. HIGH GROUND in bold cut-paper black capitals along the top edge. Small typewriter line low right.

## Page 8
Words: (none)

Picture: Morning. A wide flat green band lies across the upper third as sky, the 2 mm offset leaving a pale seam along the horizon. The field below is bare paper. Dead centre, the lawn chair stands unfolded, black frame, green webbing, and our character is asleep in it, slumped sideways, legs out, eyes drawn as two short closed curves, mouth a flat line, shoes muddy with a small green smear. One goat stands right beside the chair, flat green, black stick legs, black dot eye, head down, chewing, close enough to touch. The other three goats are in their same old row far behind, unchanged. Nothing else on the page.
