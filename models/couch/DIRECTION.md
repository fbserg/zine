UNPROMPTED, an eight-page mini zine, drawn by hand: blue ballpoint on lined paper, one yellow highlighter. Deadpan.
One recurring character carries the whole thing, page to page, like a small comic. The joke sits in the drawing. Word budget for the whole book, cover included: sixty. Eight pages, no more, no fewer.
No edition number, no price, no colophon ceremony.
It should read as ingroup to people who make and buy these things, without ever performing it. No winking. The tell is what you leave out.
This issue: a diary of one week spent trying to return a couch.
Everything else is yours: the story, the words, what is drawn, how each page is laid out.

Notes after the first print:
- A reader has to get the story on one pass, cold. If a page needs the previous page explained, the page is wrong.
- Do not fill space. Most pages want one figure, one thing, and paper. A wall of stuff is a page that lost its nerve.
- Skip anything that only exists because zines usually have it.

State your palette on the first line of the script as: Ink: <what you print with>.
