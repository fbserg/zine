Ink: blue ballpoint on ruled notebook paper, one yellow highlighter.

## Page 1
Words: UNPROMPTED (headline)

Picture: Square page of ruled paper, red margin line down the left, two punched holes on the left edge. The title UNPROMPTED sits across the top third in filled-in bubble letters, ballpoint outline, hatched inside, riding the ruled lines and running slightly downhill at the end because the writer misjudged the space. Below it, centred on bare paper, a three-seat couch drawn in flat side view with a quick confident line: square arms, three cushions, four stub legs. The cushions are filled with yellow highlighter. Nothing else on the page. No figure. No floor line under the couch, so it floats on the ruled lines like an item in a catalogue. A brown coffee ring overlaps the bottom right corner of the couch.

## Page 2
Words: MON (headline) / A couch arrived. I did not order it. (small text)

Picture: MON in bubble letters, upper left, tight against the red margin. The small text sits on the two ruled lines under it. The rest of the page is empty paper except for the lower half: an open apartment doorway drawn as a simple rectangle in ballpoint, seen straight on, and the couch wedged in it at an angle, one arm inside, one arm out, yellow cushions. Our character stands to the right of the doorway in the near-empty room, small, seen full length: short flat haircut, round glasses, striped long-sleeve shirt, arms straight down at the sides, mouth a short flat line, eyes two dots. Blank expression. The character is not touching the couch. A single ruled line serves as the floor.

## Page 3
Words: TUE (headline) / The store has no record of the couch. (small text)

Picture: TUE in bubble letters, upper left. Small text on the ruled lines beneath. The whole middle of the page is empty. At the bottom, the character sits on the floor with the back against the couch, knees up, holding a phone to one ear, cord or no cord, blank face, glasses catching two short ballpoint glints. The couch fills the bottom edge of the frame and runs off both sides, so only the front and the yellow cushion edge show. Above the character's head, three small hold-music notes drawn as plain quarter notes, spaced far apart, the last one very high on the page near the headline. No speech balloon.

## Page 4
Words: WED (headline) / They can collect it in its original packaging. (small text)

Picture: WED in bubble letters, upper left. Small text under it. Below, the character stands alone dead centre on bare paper, facing the reader, feet together, blank face. Both hands are held out in front at chest height, pinched, and between them is one short strip of clear packing tape drawn as two thin ballpoint lines with a crinkle. That is all the packaging there is. The tape strip gets the only yellow on the page, a small dash of highlighter along its length. The couch is not in the frame. Lots of ruled paper above and below the figure. A faint coffee ring in the upper right, running over the headline.

## Page 5
Words: THU (headline)

Picture: THU in bubble letters, upper left, tight against the red margin. No other words on the page. Centred: a very small two-door hatchback in flat side view, ballpoint, drawn about a third of the width of the couch. The couch is on the roof, lengthwise, yellow cushions, overhanging the front and back of the car by a couch arm at each end. Two lines of rope cross the couch and go through the open side windows. The car sits on one ruled line as road. The character is behind the wheel, visible only as a head and glasses through the side window, blank, facing forward. Small motion scratches behind the rear wheel. Nothing else on the page. The wide band of blank ruled paper above the car is the joke.

## Page 6
Words: FRI (headline) / Closed for inventory. (small text)

Picture: FRI in bubble letters, upper left. Small text under it. Centred low on the page, the storefront drawn plain and wide: a glass door, a dark window, a shut roller shutter pulled two thirds down. Taped inside the glass at eye height is a small sign, a rectangle with two scribbled lines of unreadable writing, and the sign is the only yellow on the page. The character stands facing the door with the back to the reader, hands at sides, a head and a striped shirt and two shoes, no face visible. Behind the character, at the right edge of the frame, the nose of the hatchback with the couch still on the roof, cut off by the page edge.

## Page 7
Words: SAT (headline) / I waited on the couch. (small text)

Picture: SAT in bubble letters, upper left. Small text under it. The page is mostly empty paper. Low and centre, the couch sits alone on an empty parking lot, drawn side on, yellow cushions, with two short ballpoint dashes on the ground for painted parking stall lines and one small drain grate. The character lies on the couch on the back, hands folded on the chest, shoes still on, glasses still on, eyes open, blank. Above and behind, a single streetlight on a thin pole leans in from the right edge, and the pool of light it throws on the ground is a soft oval of yellow highlighter around the couch. No stars, no moon, no buildings.

## Page 8
Words: SUN (headline) / A second couch arrived. (small text)

Picture: SUN in bubble letters, upper left. Small text under it. Same parking lot, same viewpoint as the previous page, same stall dashes. Now there are two identical couches side by side, both with yellow cushions, an arm's width apart, both facing the reader. The character sits on the left couch, upright, hands on knees, feet flat, blank face, looking straight out at the reader for the first time in the book. The right-hand couch is empty and still has a strip of packing tape stuck across one arm. The streetlight is gone. Wide empty paper above the two couches, three quarters of the page. A coffee ring sits on the bare paper between the couches and the bottom edge.
