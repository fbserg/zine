Ink: fluorescent orange

## Page 1
Words:
- Headline: UNPROMPTED
Picture: Cover. One grey pigeon stands dead centre on a thin black horizontal line, facing the reader. Her body is a rubbery black-outlined blob, small dot eyes, blank expression. A large flat orange disc of sun sits directly behind her head like a halo. The headline runs across the top in bold cut-paper black capitals. Everything else is empty off-white paper. The orange sun is misregistered about 2 mm off her outline.

## Page 2
Words:
- Small text: Dawn. The pigeon returns to her ledge.
Picture: Wide documentary shot. A flat black city skyline fills the bottom third of the page. A huge orange sun disc rises behind it. The pigeon flies across the sun in black silhouette, wings in a stiff flat V, small and centred. The narration sits in small typewriter text in the empty top-left corner.

## Page 3
Words:
- Small text: She lands.
Picture: Flat close shot of a brick wall, drawn as a black outline with a few brick lines and one window. Where a ledge used to be, there is a clean flat orange rectangle scar with four black bolt stubs. The ledge itself is gone. The pigeon sits in mid-air exactly at the level of the scar, feet tucked, wings folded, settled and content, as if the ledge is still there. Dot eyes, blank face. Nothing holds her up. The narration sits small at the bottom right.

## Page 4
Words:
- Small text: She notices.
Picture: Same wall, same orange scar, same framing as Page 3. The pigeon is now halfway down the page, still in the exact same seated pose, falling straight down. Three loose black-outlined feathers float in the air where she sat. Her beak is slightly open. Her dot eyes stay blank. The narration sits small at the bottom right, in the same place as Page 3.

## Page 5
Words:
- Small text: The neighbours have spikes.
Picture: A neighbouring stone ledge runs across the middle of the page. Along its top is a strip of anti-bird spikes, printed in orange. The pigeon stands on top of the spike points, legs stiff and wide apart, perfectly upright and perfectly still, balanced like a dinner guest who will not complain. Blank dot eyes look straight at the reader. The narration sits small in the empty upper half.

## Page 6
Words:
- Small text: Then she sees it.
Picture: A lot of empty sky. At the bottom left, the pigeon stands tiny on a black pavement line next to one orange traffic cone, her head tilted up. Far away in the top right corner, a small orange construction crane holds a black slab on its hook. The slab has four bolt stubs on one side: it is her ledge. The narration sits small in the centre of the empty sky.

## Page 7
Words:
- Small text: She reclaims her territory.
Picture: High in the air. The ledge slab hangs from an orange crane hook by two black chains that run off the top of the page. The pigeon sits on the slab in the same settled, feet-tucked pose as Page 3, content, dot eyes blank. Tiny flat black rooftops lie far below at the bottom of the page. The narration sits small in the empty space beside the slab.

## Page 8
Words:
- Small text: Where the ledge goes, she goes.
Picture: A straight road runs from the bottom of the page to a vanishing point at the centre. A black flatbed truck drives away from the reader down the road. The ledge slab lies on the back of the truck, and the pigeon sits on it, facing backwards toward the reader, settled, blank. A low orange sun disc sits on the horizon at the end of the road. The rest is empty paper. The narration sits small at the bottom centre.
