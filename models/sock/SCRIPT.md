Ink: fluorescent pink

## Page 1
Words:
- headline: UNPROMPTED

Picture: Cover. A front-loading washing machine fills the lower half of the square, drawn as a flat black outline with a big round porthole. Behind the glass the drum is solid black. A thick fold of the rubber door seal sits at the bottom of the porthole, and one sock pokes out of a gap in that fold, top half only. The sock is solid pink with a black toe, black heel and black cuff band. Two small black dot eyes sit just under the cuff. Its expression is blank. The headline UNPROMPTED runs across the top in black cut-paper capitals, with the pink layer shifted 2 mm off the black. The rest is empty off-white paper.

## Page 2
Words:
- small text: Wash day.

Picture: Two identical socks sit side by side on the rim of a plain black-outlined laundry basket in the centre of the page. Both are solid pink with a black toe, black heel and black cuff band, two dot eyes each, blank faces. They stand upright like small bodies, bent at the heel like knees. The toe of the left sock rests on top of the toe of the right sock. No other objects. Small typewriter text sits under the basket. Wide empty paper all around.

## Page 3
Words:
- small text: The machine takes one.

Picture: Close view of the porthole, a large black circle filling most of the page. Inside, pink suds float as flat round blobs on the black drum. At the bottom edge the black rubber seal folds open like a mouth, and one pink sock is half gone into the fold. Only its cuff and two dot eyes still show, facing out, blank. The second pink sock is pressed flat against the inside of the glass, toe spread, dot eyes looking straight at the reader, equally blank. Small typewriter text sits in the paper margin below.

## Page 4
Words:
- headline: THE SOCK DIMENSION

Picture: A flat pink sky fills the top two thirds of the page. A thin black horizon line crosses the page. Below it, off-white paper ground. Near the top of the pink sky is a small black hole shaped like the gap in a rubber seal. The pink sock falls from it, straight down, toe first, cuff up, dot eyes calm. Far off on the horizon stand dozens of tiny single socks, drawn in black outline only, each a different pattern: dots, stripes, diamonds, plain. The headline sits in black cut-paper capitals on the paper ground at the bottom.

## Page 5
Words:
- small text: Everyone here holds a picture of the one they lost.

Picture: A crowd of single socks stands in rows, drawn in black outline on bare paper, each with a different black pattern: polka dot, stripe, argyle, plain white, one with a hole at the toe. Every sock holds up a small framed picture with a stubby rubbery arm. Each picture shows a sock exactly identical to the one holding it, same pattern, same dot eyes. In the front row centre stands the pink sock, the only pink figure on the page. It holds up a picture of a pink sock with a black toe, heel and cuff. All faces are blank. Small typewriter text runs along the bottom.

## Page 6
Words:
- small text: Nobody ever comes.

Picture: The pink sock sits alone on a small grey-black lump of lint in the middle of empty paper. Its picture frame hangs from one arm and touches the ground. Its cuff droops forward and folds over a little. Dot eyes look down. Three tiny black dust bunnies sit near its toe. Nothing else on the page. The pink is only the sock and the small picture in the frame. Small typewriter text sits low on the page.

## Page 7
Words:
- small text: Then one came.

Picture: The top of the page is flat pink sky with the same small black seal-shaped hole as page 4. A second pink sock with a black toe, heel and cuff shoots out of the hole in a perfect diver pose, body straight, toe pointed down, stubby arms pressed flat to its sides. Its dot eyes are open and its face is blank. A few flat pink suds blobs trail behind it. At the bottom edge of the page, very small, the first pink sock sits on its lint lump and looks up, face blank. Small typewriter text sits in the empty middle.

## Page 8
Words:
- small text: Nobody put it in the wash.
- small text: It climbed in.

Picture: The two pink socks sit side by side on the lint lump, same pose as page 2, the toe of one resting on the toe of the other. Both faces are blank. Behind them, the whole crowd of black-outline single socks from page 5 stands in rows and stares at the pair, dot eyes all pointed at them. Every crowd sock still holds its framed self-portrait, and one plain white sock in the back row lowers its picture to look. The first line of small typewriter text sits above the pair. The second line sits below them, alone on the paper.
