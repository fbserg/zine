Two-colour risograph print on uncoated off-white paper. Exactly two inks: black and the second ink named in the script. The second ink layer is misregistered about 2 mm from the black. Flat shapes only, no gradients, no crosshatching, no halftone photos, no shading. Slight riso grain. Deadpan art-comic drawing: rubbery simplified bodies, small dot eyes, blank expressions, thick even black outlines, generous empty paper. Square page. Text is part of the print: bold cut-paper capitals for headlines, small typewriter for everything else, every letter legible and spelled exactly.
