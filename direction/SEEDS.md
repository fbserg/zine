How to write one
- Say the situation in a dozen words, then stop.
- Give a situation, not a plan: no tone notes, no page instructions, no shape for the ending.
- A negative earns its place only when it is the joke.
- Name one thing the editor would not have picked. Let it supply the rest.
- Place is optional and caps at one line.
